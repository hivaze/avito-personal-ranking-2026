WHAT THE STUDY TELLS US (descriptive; TPE sampling is not random, so the group means are confounded)
1. Regression to the mean in the baseline: the n2cpv3 pool's FT gain over TO-only is -0.00016 on TUNE and +0.00054
   on CONFIRM (+0.00019 on all val).
   - Any different FT realization therefore looks ~0.0005 better on TUNE than on CONFIRM.
   - Across all 35 complete trials, mean d_TUNE is +0.00029 and mean d_CONFIRM -0.00024.
   - Lesson: an FT objective on half of val, against a single fixed FT realization, carries a sizeable
     baseline-luck term. The CONFIRM half was essential; every TUNE number above +0.0005 was inflated by it.
2. TUNE and CONFIRM deltas correlate +0.81 across trials. Config effects are real, but the large ones are the
   HARMFUL configs:
   - 6 epochs at lr >= 1.2e-4 with EMA (trials 14, 20, 22, 30): CONFIRM -0.0015 to -0.0043.
   - ema 0.99 configs average CONFIRM -0.0012.
   The upside region is flat and small.
3. The upside region is consistent: preset none (no extra-feature branch), loss_norm feed, const lr 4e-5 to 1e-4,
   4-6 epochs, ema 0 or 0.995.
   - Trials 21, 33, 15, 25, 28, 19, 34, 27 all give CONFIRM +0.0004 to +0.0005, each about 1.0-1.3 SE.
   - These are near-duplicates, not independent evidence. Set B shows they do not generalize to other members.
4. The extra-feature branch (n2c_pre_v3, 42 features) no longer helps on m02 trunks: preset none is >= v3 in this
   study. If the FT ever has to be re-run for cost reasons, dropping the branch is a free simplification. This is
   not a gain claim.
5. loss_norm feed (the trunks' own normalisation) alone is null. The control (trial 8) gives TUNE +0.00010 +-
   0.00031 and CONFIRM +0.00023 +- 0.00028.
6. fANOVA importance on the TUNE objective: ema 0.23, seeds 0.18, epochs 0.18, lr 0.15, wd 0.09, preset 0.09,
   decay 0.04, drop 0.02, loss_norm 0.02.
7. This adds to the lead's L2 finding: the FT half adds ~0 at pool level on m02 trunks, and retuning the FT recipe
   does not change that. The val labels are worth ~+0.0002-0.0008 over the TO-only pool whichever reasonable FT is
   used. The members are the limit.
