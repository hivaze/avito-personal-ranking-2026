=== 2026-11-15T04:06:27Z R7: CALIBRATION OF THE A2 BARS (no twin exists; written before insample_gap.py exists or runs) ===
Why: A2 is the sharp check, and its bars (+0.005 full retrain, +0.002 re-cool; R4) are inferred. The only measured analogue so
far is the TEACHER ensemble (0.344 on feeds its members trained on, 0.324 on the holdout). A student differs: half of its
target is the teacher, and on the holdout rows a twin's teacher half is OUT of sample, so the twin's in-sample lift on the
holdout comes through the label half only. If the bar is above what a correct twin shows, A2 raises a false DEFECT.
Measurement (insample_gap.py, CPU, existing checkpoints only; no twin involved):
    G_s = NDCG of an ORIGINAL final member (@last checkpoint) on 20,000 exploration-like TRAINING feeds drawn by the holdout
    rule from the feeds that are not in the holdout (p_random > 0.9, M >= 50, rng 777; in sample for the member, teacher
    in sample too) MINUS its out-of-sample NDCG on the 30,000 holdout feeds (its saved @last holdout file). Unpaired SE.
    Members: w5_e10_kd05w192_s951 (W16) and abl_e10_pop1kd05t25_s951 (popularity-light, W8P20). Path check: the same CPU
    code re-predicts 2,000 holdout feeds; their NDCG must agree with the saved GPU file's on those feeds within 0.002 and the
    per-feed-centred scores must correlate > 0.999, otherwise the measurement is void and R4's bars stay.
    Null noise of the A2 statistic: s_null = sqrt(2) x (SD over the 16 W16 originals of their @last holdout NDCG, logs).
Rule (bars can only go DOWN from R4, never below 3 s_null; G_s = the smaller of the two members' values):
    full retrain:  bar_F = max(3 s_null, min(0.005, 0.25 G_s))
    re-cool:       bar_R = max(3 s_null, min(0.002, 0.10 G_s))
    Reasoning behind 0.25 and 0.10 [I]: the label half of the target is 0.5, so a full-retrain twin is expected near 0.5 G_s
    and the bar sits at half of that; a re-cool twin has 2 of 10 passes at 35% of the peak learning rate, expected 0.2 to
    0.5 of the full lift, bar at 0.10 G_s.
    accept_twins.py reads logs/a2_calib.json when it exists, prints the R4 bar and the bar in force, and judges A2 by the
    bar in force. If the measurement is void or absent, R4's bars are in force.
