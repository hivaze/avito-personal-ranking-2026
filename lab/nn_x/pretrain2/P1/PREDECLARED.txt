PROTOCOL (tests 1 and 2)
- Nested: user-grouped 5-fold (user 0 = singletons), 2 fold seeds (0, 1), per-feed deltas averaged over the 2 seeds.
- Global arm: the grid point maximising mean NDCG on the training folds. Per-cluster arm: for each cluster the grid point
  maximising mean NDCG on that cluster's training-fold feeds; raw (argmax) and shrunk (objective (S_c(w) + tau * mean_all(w)) / (n_c + tau),
  tau = 1000 feeds) variants. Clusters with no training-fold feed fall back to the global choice.
- val: selection and evaluation on the leak-free 14,306 feeds (qt.leak_mask); holdout: all 30,000 feeds, nested within the holdout.
- Primary contrast: per-feed delta = NDCG(per-cluster nested) - NDCG(global nested); mean +- SE (sd/sqrt(n)).
