DEFINITIONS (fixed now)

- History set per feed: valid history events (hist_emb_idx >= 0), all H <= 256, de-duplicated by item_id keeping the most recent occurrence.
- Fit (train table only, fixed, label-free): <= 5M distinct train items sampled from train history events + train candidate rows, category from
  the flat row. mu_k = mean unit vector of category k; Sigma_k = within-category covariance; Sigma_pool = n-weighted mean of Sigma_k;
  shrunk Sigma~_k = (n_k Sigma_k + 5000 Sigma_pool) / (n_k + 5000); W_k = (Sigma~_k + lam_k I)^(-1/2), lam_k = 0.1 * tr(Sigma~_k)/128.
- S0 raw   = cos(c, h).
- S1 (PRIMARY, gated) = cos(W_k (c - mu_k), W_k (h - mu_k)), k = the CANDIDATE's category (per-category centre + per-category shrunk ZCA).
- S1g (secondary, not gated) = same with the pooled within-category W (one W, per-category mu).
- S2 CSLS (secondary, not gated) = 2 cos(c,h) - rC(c) - rH(h); rC(c) = mean of the top-10 raw cos of c to a fixed pool of 50K distinct
  train history items (event-sampled); rH(h) = mean top-10 raw cos of h to a fixed pool of 50K distinct train candidate items (row-sampled).
- Candidate signal (primary) = max over the feed's history set of the similarity (maxsim_all). Secondary signals: top-16 mean, max over
  the last-32 valid events, rec_hl3 (weights exp(-k/3) over valid events, k = 0 newest), mean of last 5 valid events.
