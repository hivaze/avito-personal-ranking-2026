DATA / CORPUS FOR f(history)  (label-free)
- Fit corpus: 200,000 random train feeds (np.random.default_rng(7)) with user_id != 0, H >= 1, ||u|| > 0 and not in the
  holdout set, PLUS the 30,000 holdout feeds (nn_x/r6/train.py rule: rng 12345 over p_random > 0.9 & M >= 50; holdout
  feeds with user_id 0 / H 0 / ||u|| 0 are not fitted and get no residual).
- Cross-fitting: GroupKFold(5, shuffle, random_state=0) by user_id over that corpus. Fold model k is fitted on 4/5 of
  the users and predicts the remaining 1/5 (out-of-fold). Holdout feeds get their OOF prediction; val (and test, if
  computed) get the mean of the 5 fold models. R^2 (pooled over the 128 dims of the L2-normalised u) is reported per fold.
- Target: u / ||u|| (int8 user_emb, own feed only; never another snapshot of the same user).
- Inputs (own feed's history only, own split's item table, items L2-normalised, emb_idx -1 skipped):
  mean of all events; recency-weighted mean (weight 0.5^(hpos/8)); last1; mean of last 5 / 20 / 32 positions;
  per-event-type means over the last 64 positions for et in {1},{2},{3},{4},{5..10}; category histogram (54 bins)
  of the last 64 and of all events (shares); event-type shares (10); log1p(H); missing-content share; a zero-filled
  block gets an indicator. Features standardised on the fit fold.
- Ridge lambda (on standardised inputs) chosen from {1,10,100,1000,10000} by mean OOF R^2 (label-free choice).
- r = u/||u|| - f(h); nr = ||r||.

RESIDUAL TEST ON G3 (nested, val)
- Base: zG3 = per-feed z of preds/ens_G3_final_val.npy.
- Population: leak-free val feeds (qt.leak_mask, 14,306). Feeds with user_id 0, ||u|| 0 or H 0 are EXCLUDED from all
  fitting and keep G3 unchanged (delta 0).
- Features (per candidate; missing candidate content -> 0 after NaN-aware feed z):
  x1 = z(cos_u)   [cos_u = cos(candidate int8 emb, user_emb), recomputed and checked vs data/feats cos_u]
  x2 = nr_s * z(cos_u)
  x3 = nr_s * z(cos(c, r))
  nr_s = (nr - mean)/sd over the non-excluded feeds of the split (label-free).
- PRIMARY fit (3-parameter ridge): within-feed-demeaned least squares of y on [zG3, x1, x2, x3], zG3 unpenalised,
  ridge penalty 0.01 * N_rows on the x coefficients; weights w = beta_x / beta_zG3; score = zG3 + X w.
  Fitted on 4/5 of the users (GroupKFold(5, shuffle) by user, user-0 singletons, fold seeds 0 and 1), evaluated on the
  held-out 1/5; per-feed delta = mean over the 2 fold seeds; paired per-feed NDCG delta vs G3 (src/metrics.ndcg_per_feed).
- Holdout replica: identical nested procedure on the 30,000 holdout feeds, base = member-equal per-feed z-mean of the 20
  G3 train-only members' holdout preds (F10 v23_gru_pre2s_s501-506, F7x r6_cd_m02_r101-107, F7x70 r6_cd_m02_p70k_r101-107;
  must reproduce 0.32379), f via OOF cross-fit. Info only: full-val-fit weights applied to the holdout (transfer).
- SE = sd(per-feed delta)/sqrt(n_feeds) over the gate population.
