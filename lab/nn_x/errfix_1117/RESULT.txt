ERROR ANALYSIS OF THE FINAL MODEL: CODE RULES TO CORRECT ITS MISTAKES (2026-11-17)

Question (owner, 2026-11-17): analyse the errors our model makes on validation and holdout, find heuristics in
the features, and turn them into code rules that correct the wrong predictions.

Design (fixed before any analysis; ledger entry 2026-11-17T01:37:42Z)
- code/build_tables.py: one row per candidate, 66 columns: the FD16P20 score and its two branches (s_ft,
  s_to), rank, score gaps, f1..f5, home region, cosines to the user and to the history (all, newest 1/5/10/32,
  mean of 5, by event type: contact, order, favourite, lead, feed view, search view), near-copies in the
  history and among candidates, the user's history in the candidate's category, category size and rank in
  the feed, popularity, exposure, co-visitation, encoder scores, feed-level context. Own-feed data only.
- DISCOVERY: validation users of one half, 7,214 feeds (FD16P20 NDCG 0.39939), the only labelled sample the
  analysts saw (code/kit.py). CONFIRM: the other half by user, 7,209 feeds (0.40604), sealed. HOLDOUT: the
  30,000 random-order train feeds scored by W8P20's train-only branch, whose members never trained on them
  (0.32393), sealed. Test table built for application only.
- Pass rule: CONFIRM delta >= +0.0002 and z at or above the Holm threshold over the submitted rules; holdout
  veto at z <= -2.5 (except popularity-only rules); passing rules combined and re-confirmed; only then a test
  file by code.
- Workflow a workflow run: 8 Opus 5.5 analysts (xhigh), CPU only, one lens each: head of the list,
  categories, inside categories and near-duplicates, feed segments and score shape, recent intent from the
  history, popularity / freshness / cold, a learned residual model as an upper bound, and a feed-by-feed
  deep dive of confident errors. 1.77M tokens, 38 min. Audit of all 478 tool calls and every file they
  wrote (code/audit.py): 0 touches of the sealed data, label files or prediction files.

Result: NOTHING PASSES. CLOSED.
- About 9,900 rule variants in about 900 families were scored by cross-fitted NDCG on discovery. 7 of 8
  analysts submitted no rule. One weak rule was submitted (small feeds under 40 candidates: + 0.05 z(hmax);
  discovery +0.00074 +- 0.00031, best of 144 pairs). CONFIRM -0.00038 +- 0.00028 (z -1.36): no pass
  (confirm_round1.json).
- Exploratory read, declared ineligible beforehand: the two unsubmitted near-misses with rule files read
  +0.00003 (z 0.39) and +0.00005 (z 0.19) on CONFIRM, -0.00001 and -0.00011 on HOLDOUT
  (confirm_exploratory.json).

What the analysts established (analysts_result.json, agents/*/notes.txt)
- The score is a calibrated log-odds: a within-feed softmax of it has slope 0.98-1.04; observed/expected
  positive rates stay within 0.93-1.07 across bins of every recency, similarity, category, popularity,
  freshness and exposure column, and every leaf category has |z| <= 2.2.
- Learned correctors cannot beat it out of fold: LightGBM residual models on 130 columns, cross-fitted,
  reach at best +0.0004 (z 0.8) and lose up to -0.012 as they grow, while in-sample they gain +0.037.
  Linear conditional logits lose 0.001-0.011. This bounds what any rule on these columns can gain at about 0.
- Head: no subgroup of 308 bins where rank 2 beats rank 1; the s_ft / s_to mix is optimal at 0.5 overall,
  in the head and in every segment; where the branches disagree, their blend is right most often.
- Near-copies do not split clicks (the lower copy is slightly under-, not over-estimated), so there is
  nothing for diversification to fix.
- Real likelihood residuals exist (category size and share, recency in large feeds, Services slightly
  over-ranked: O/E 0.93) but sit in the middle and tail of the list or are feed-level, and do not change
  NDCG.
- The feeds the model gets wrong are large (median 167 candidates vs 29), and their positives are the
  12th-or-so item of a large category or exploration clicks with low similarity to recent history. Nothing
  in the columns separates them; choices driven by photo or price would look like this.
- Item prior from validation labels: items positive for another user are positive 1.79x as often, but cover
  0.19% of rows (0.33% of test rows); best +0.00057 (z 1.82).
