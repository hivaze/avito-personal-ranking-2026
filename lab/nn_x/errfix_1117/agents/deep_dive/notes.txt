DEEP_DIVE AGENT - ERROR ANALYSIS OF FD16P20 ON DISCOVERY (7,214 feeds, 697,304 rows). 2026-11-17
Lens: confidently wrong cases, read one by one. CPU only, 12 threads. Scripts in scripts/ (a01..a20).

BOTTOM LINE
- The confident errors of the model (rank 1 negative with a large gap to rank 2; positives ranked 50+ in small feeds)
  are not systematic for any feature or segment I could find. In every head segment I tested, rank 1 beats
  rank 2, and rank-1 precision rises with the gap (top gap quintile: 20.3% vs 8.3% for rank 2). At matched
  score, positives and negatives do not differ on any column beyond noise.
- One weak rule submitted (rules/smallfeed_hmax.py): cross-fitted +0.00074, SE 0.00031, z 2.39. It was the best
  of 144 segment x feature pairs, so after selection it is probably noise. It is up to the lead's sealed check.
- Item prior from validation labels (the data idea): real but small. Cross-fitted +0.0003 to +0.0006, z 1.1 to 1.8.
  Below the bar on discovery, and it needs labels when applied.
- Diagnostics: (1) A residual LightGBM boosted from the model's own score (init_score = 0.98*score, the fitted
  within-feed softmax slope), cross-fitted by user, gains at most +0.00023 (z 0.6). Larger versions lose
  (-0.0010 to -0.0025). (2) A free stacker on all columns loses -0.006 to -0.011. So there is no learnable
  residual structure in these columns at this sample size, and the model is close to calibrated on every
  single column.

  * Rank 1 is very often in the category of the newest event (cat_newest_idx 0) and a near-copy of a recent
    event (h1/h5 ~.95-1.0), often with many near-duplicates in history (n_hdup95 5-79). Examples: 14380, 11656, 418.
  * A recurring sub-type is a brand-new, cold listing at rank 1: xp_imp_c50 = 0, f4 < .1, f5 ~ 0, often s_ft > s_to.
    Examples: 315, 7536, 10645, 1202, 12210, 6240, 10648, 5726.
  * In 35% of these feeds the positive is in the same category as rank 1 but deep inside it (within-category
    loss). 11% are in a category absent from the history (exploration). Median best rank is 10 over all
    confident-wrong feeds.
In lens (b): positives are mostly exploration items (category absent from history, cat_n_in_feed 1-3, f1 at
  its floor) or deep members of the main category. Nothing distinguishes them from their neighbours in rank.
In the small feeds: the positives at ranks 8-22 look like their neighbours.

BEHAVIOURAL HYPOTHESES AND TESTS (all on all of discovery)
H1 "The model is overconfident about fresh, cold listings at the top" (lens a sub-type).
   In confident feeds, a fresh-cold rank 1 has precision 12.5% (n=345) vs 20.3% (n=1459) for other rank 1s.
   But its rank 2 is worse still (7.8%), and in no cell does rank 2 beat rank 1 (head_wide table, a07/a19).
   At the same score level, fresh-cold items are calibrated: O/E 0.94-1.03 in every score band (a20).
   Shrinking high scores of fresh/cold items: cross-fitted -0.0001 to -0.0003 (6 forms).
   Demoting fresh-cold items in the top 3: -0.00035. REJECTED.
H2 "Near-copies of history are saturated: the user has seen this listing type many times" (n_hdup95 large).
   O/E is above 1 (1.05-1.6), not below. Demotion and boost are both <= 0 cross-fitted. REJECTED.
H3 "Near-duplicate candidates split one click between them".
   I rebuilt 56,550 near-duplicate pairs (same feed, category and cand_maxcos). 73 pairs have both members
   positive, against about 12 expected under independence. Users who like one copy click both, so the copies
   complement each other. Demoting the second member of a pair: cross-fitted -0.00037 (z -2.4). REJECTED.
H4 "The model under-uses the current-session category (same_cat_newest) at the head".
   O/E 1.09 at rank 1 vs 0.88 for non-matching items (z about 2). But at the head, every swap or boost of
   matching items loses: the best weight in-sample is negative, -0.05. REJECTED.
H5 Session-type segments (search session, after contact, after favourite, after order, after lead, guest, short
   history, focused vs diverse user, night, small feed) x 12 item features (h1, hmax, same_cat_newest,
   popularity, cold, freshness, ucos, c_order, c_contact, n_hdup95>0, category absent, f1).
   144 pairs x 8 weights, cross-fitted. Best: small feed (M<40) x hmax, +0.00074 z 2.39, and guest x n_hdup95,
   +0.00017 z 2.39. Worst: after_fav x hmax, z -3.1, and small feed x freshness, z -3.0. With 144 pairs, a
   largest |z| near 3 is what chance alone gives. Only small_feed x hmax clears the brief's thresholds.
H6 "Branch disagreement = uncertainty; the probability is convex in the score, so uncertain items deserve a bonus".
   |s_ft - s_to| bonus: -0.00035. Power mean of exp scores: -0.00005. Weight a*s_ft + (1-a)*s_to: -0.00025
   (a=0.5 is already optimal). Rank-disagreement bonus: -0.00046. REJECTED.
H7 "The same user is shown an item again after ignoring it". Not testable: 6,872 of 6,887 users have a single
   feed in discovery, and only 60 rows are repeat impressions (0 positives).
H8 Global single-column corrections (28 forms from the O/E scan: f1, f2 bands, category absent, hmax bands,
   hmax_type 4, hmax_idx, n_cand_dup95, ucos, seq_cv, cold, fresh, f5, cat_hist_share, ...): all cross-fitted
   between -0.0009 and +0.0003; none has z > 1.5. The O/E deviations seen with a coarse rank-cell calibration
   mostly vanish once the expectation is calibrated per feed (the number of positives differs between feeds).
   Within-feed matched comparison (each positive vs its +/-3 rank neighbours) for ranks 4+: no column beyond |z| 3.1.
   About 45 columns x 4 regions were scanned, so that is noise level.

DATA IDEA: ITEM-LEVEL PRIOR FROM DISCOVERY LABELS (a08, a08b)
- In discovery, 11.5% of rows show an item that also appears in another discovery feed. 0.24% of rows show an
  item that was positive in another feed. On test: 12.7% and 0.33%.
- Counting out of fold (counts from the other 4 user folds, as for a new population):
  * Items with a prior positive: 1,327 rows, 47 positives vs 26.2 expected (O/E 1.79).
  * Items seen before but never clicked: O/E 1.04 (a weak popularity signal).
  * Items never seen: O/E 0.99.
- Rules, cross-fitted:
  * score + 0.5*log(smoothed rate / base): +0.00057, SE 0.00031, z 1.82.
  * The same on prior positives only: +0.0003 to +0.0004, z 1.1 to 1.6.
  * Impression count alone: negative.
- Judgement: real (O/E 1.8) but tiny on discovery. On test the prior would be built from all 14.4k validation
  feeds, about 2.5x more than the 4/5 of discovery used here, so coverage of prior positives rises to about
  0.33% of rows. That suggests a gain on the order of +0.0005 to +0.001 at most. It also assumes the item's
  appeal carries over in time, and test is a later period, so it may decay. It needs validation labels when
  applied, so it cannot be a column-only rule. Not submitted. It is worth a quick check by the lead, who can
  build the prior from all validation and measure it on the train-period holdout (no overlap there) or on a
  time split.
