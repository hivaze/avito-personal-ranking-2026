1. CALIBRATION ALONG THE LENS (expected = npos * softmax(score) within the feed; temperature 1.0 is the ML fit)
- One-dimensional residuals are flat. A/E for xp_imp_c50 bins ranges 0.94-1.02, f4 0.89-1.07, f5 0.97-1.08,
  it_hist_* 0.93-1.07, seq_cv 0.94-1.04, pre2_fe/new 0.94-1.14. No |z| > 2.7 in 100+ cells.
- The only clear miscalibration is a temperature effect: the head is over-confident (A/E 0.934 at rank 1, 0.946 at
  ranks 2-3, 1.064 at ranks 11-20). That does not change the order, so it cannot be exploited.
- f4 < 0.2 implies cold: young listings have no train impressions (xp = 0) in 99.9% of rows. So "fresh" and "cold"
  are the same set for young items. On test, with popularity 11 days stale, the cold set will also include older
  items. That is one more reason not to lean on cold status.
- f5 reliability: high f5 at low exposure is NOT over-trusted. For f5 >= .8 and xp < 1.5, A/E is 1.01 at rank 1,
  0.97 at ranks 2-3, 1.04 at ranks 4-10 and 1.12 at ranks 11-30. At saturated exposure (50 impressions), f5 looks
  slightly under-used (head A/E 1.25-1.50 for f5 >= .8, 0.77-0.92 for f5 < .4), but no tilt survives a
  cross-fit (section 3).
- Encoder scores on cold items: pre2 is not mis-trusted on cold items. Corr(score, pre2) is 0.70 on cold items
  and 0.64 on warm ones; A/E is flat by pre2 bin within cold and within warm items.

2. UPPER-BOUND PROBES (if a flexible learner cannot gain on these columns, simple rules cannot either)
- LightGBM lambdarank with init_score = score (lens + rank/score/context): OOF -0.0004 to -0.0067 (worse with
  more rounds).
- LightGBM without score features. Binary objective with init = calibrated logit: best +0.00049 (z 0.94), the
  rest -0.0013 to +0.0003. Lambdarank: best +0.00032 (z 0.79), mostly negative.
- Regularised conditional logit with offset = score, cross-fitted over L2 strength:
  lens main effects (19 features): best +0.00017 (z 0.8)
  + category interactions (159): best -0.00007
  + segment interactions (100): best +0.00004
  all (240): best -0.00005
  Even IN-SAMPLE the lens-only fit changes NDCG by -0.0004. Likelihood gains are calibration, not ordering.
- Head-only conditional logit (softmax over the top-5/10/20, re-rank only inside the head): OOF -0.0002 to -0.0024.
- Branch mix a*s_ft + (1-a)*s_to: a = 0.5 is optimal (a = 0.4: -0.00014; 0.6: +0.00001; 0.7: -0.00050). No
  feed segment (cold share, fresh share, M, H, guest) prefers another mix in a stable way. Item-level
  (s_to - s_ft) * g for g in {cold, young, sat, low-exposure, old, f5 < .1, all}: best CF +0.00020 (z 1.74).

4. THE NEAR-MISS (work/rejected_cold_heavycat.py; NOT submitted)
   score + 0.09 * [xp_imp_c50 == 0 and cat_views >= 50]. Reads xp_imp_c50 (cold status), cat_views, score.
   kit.check_rule in-sample +0.00068 (se 0.00027, z 2.52), 28% of feeds reordered. Reasons for rejection:
   the nested check is negative; 10 feeds carry all of the net gain (386 feeds better, 533 worse); the response
   is jagged in the threshold (cv>=20 +0.00011, >=50 +0.00054, >=100 +0.00006) and in the weight (.06 +.00037,
   .09 +.00068, .12 +.00024, .15 +.00004); the young-cold part is negative (-0.00018), only old-cold is positive;
   the expected gain is below the bar even if the effect is real; and the cold set shifts on test.
   Behavioural reading: heavy browsers of a category have already been shown its established listings, so
   never-shown ones are novel. Pairwise, flipped near-ties favour the boosted item 55% overall and 57% in the head
   (n = 255). The direction is plausibly right; the size is not worth a rule.

5. INDIVIDUAL FEEDS (work/a05_feeds.py)
   Good feeds (positive at ranks 1-3): the head is the user's top category, often near-copies (hmax >= .95) of
   recent views. The clicked item and the top negatives have the same age, exposure and f5 profile.
   Bad feeds (best positive at 30+): mostly large feeds (mean M 174 vs 57), 15.7 feed categories vs 9.5. Typical
   miss: the positive is in a lower-ranked or long-unvisited category (exploration), with ordinary lens values
   (median xp 1.39, f4 .51, f5 .44, near the all-row medians). No lens column separates these positives from the
   negatives above them: medians of positives at 30+ vs negatives at 1-3 are xp 1.39 vs 1.79, f4 .51 vs .56,
   f5 .46 vs .57, and the model already uses these differences.
   Guest feeds (4.3% of feeds): fresh items are over-predicted (A/E .52), but they sit far from the top, so the
   NDCG effect is +0.00001.
