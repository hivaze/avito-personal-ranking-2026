CATEGORY LENS: ERROR ANALYSIS OF FD16P20 ON THE DISCOVERY HALF (7,214 feeds, 697,304 rows), 2026-11-17
=====================================================================================================

RESULT: NO RULE SUBMITTED. rules/ is empty on purpose.
Across about 377 rule families (about 2,650 parameter variants, all scored by cross-fitted NDCG over kit.folds),
no category rule reached a cross-fitted gain of +0.0003 with z >= 2. The best honest numbers were between
+0.0002 and +0.0004, with z between 1.0 and 2.0. Given how many families were tried, those are what noise
looks like.

HOW I MEASURED
- Calibration baseline: a conditional logit inside each feed. Expected positives per row = npos(feed) *
  softmax(b . g(score, rank)), where g = [score, three hinges at 1/2/3, log rank, min(gap_top,.5), rank==1,
  rank<=3]. With only the score the slope is 0.98. O/E and feed-clustered score-test z by group.
- A LambdaRank directional-gradient screen (cu.lambda_test), and head "duels" (adjacent ranks r/r+1 with
  exactly one positive, logistic on the score gap plus a feature difference, SEs clustered by feed).
- Every rule family: new = score + w*g(x). w was chosen on 4 folds and scored on the 5th (cu.Ctx.crossfit).
  Cross-fitted (cf) delta, SE and z are reported over all 7,214 feeds.
- Helper code: cu.py. Analysis scripts: a01..a22 (*.py). a11 results: a11_results.csv.

CALIBRATION FINDINGS (within-feed O/E against the flexible baseline; z is feed-clustered)
1. Category-level calibration is good. By vertical, O/E runs from 0.93 (Services) to 1.03 (Electronics).
   By leaf category every |z| <= 2.2. A conditional logit over the categories of each feed gives a log-mass
   coefficient of 1.00-1.04, so the model's category mass is calibrated.
2. Services is the most consistent over-rank: O/E 0.93 overall (z -2.2), 0.72 when absent from history
   (46 vs 64, z -2.2) and 0.73 when cat_views == 0. It is also strong in the lambda screen
   (cat_user_rank x Services z -4.45). As an NDCG rule it is too small: a global "-0.1 on Services" gives
   cf +0.00022, SE 0.00022, z 1.0. w=0.1 was chosen in all 5 folds, but the per-fold deltas were
   +.00063 / -.00008 / +.00011 / +.00014 / +.00029. Gating the demotion on weak Services history makes it
   worse (cat_views==0: cf -0.00015; views<=2: cf -0.00023).
3. Same-category-as-newest at the head: at ranks 1-3 the O/E is 1.08 for items in the newest event's
   category against 0.87-0.95 for other categories (z +3.0 / -2.9). Every rule built on it is negative out
   of fold: boost inside the top K for K in {2,3,5,10,all} gives cf -0.0001 to -0.0004; conditioned on
   n_hist_cats32, newest_type, newest_src or the vertical of the newest event (18 segments), the best is
   cf +0.00038 at z 1.6 (nt=1, w=-0.05, the opposite sign).
4. Feed share of the category (cat_n_in_feed/M) is under-used by likelihood (score test z +5.6, lambda z
   +5.0, duel z +2.8 at R=3). The feed composition carries the production recommender's belief. NDCG does
   not move: share boost cf -0.00064; 0.2*log cat_n cf +0.00013 (z 1.1); head-only K=3..30 cf -0.0002.
   The likelihood gain sits in the tail.
5. Head concentration is a feed-level temperature effect only. When the top 10 is a single category, the
   head is under-confident (O/E 1.14, z +3.4) and the tail over-confident. When the head is diverse (5-7
   categories), the opposite holds. Within-feed order does not change with temperature, so no rule exists
   here. It does argue against diversification: the second item of the dominant category at ranks 1-3 has
   O/E 1.10.
6. "Actions but zero views" in a category is over-ranked for Services (O/E 0.52), Cars (0.44) and Clothing
   (0.69), but not overall (O/E 0.96-1.0). Apartments goes the other way (1.49).
7. Context x vertical (hour, weekday, guest, H, M): 118 cells. The extremes are Services at 12-17h (O/E 0.82,
   z -3.5) and Services in M>=200 feeds (0.77, z -3.3). NDCG cf for the top 8 cells ranges from -0.00008 to
   +0.00031. H50-199 x Services gives +0.00031 at z 2.03, but it was picked post hoc from 118 cells and has
   no behavioural story, so it is not credible.
8. Individual bad feeds (best positive at 30+). The positive is usually deep inside a category the model
   already favours (e.g. Clothing 24th of 164, Parts 68th of 100, Services 11th of 50, Clothing 66th of 90),
   with lower hmax/ucos than the head and unremarkable popularity. These are within-category picks driven
   by the photo or price, which the features cannot see. Fewer cases are a second-recent category (Vacancies
   at cat_newest_idx 1, behind 120 Apartments led by a favourite) or an absent category (Sports). Good feeds
   are mostly the newest or #1 category with a near-copy or high-similarity item at the top.

TAKEAWAYS FOR THE LEAD
- On this sample the category structure is used close to optimally: offsets, branch weights, slopes and
  feature re-weighting by vertical or category state all fail out of fold.
- Likelihood and LambdaRank-surrogate signals (feed share, history share, f3, Services) are real in the
  likelihood sense but sit at ranks that do not move NDCG. Several of them make NDCG worse even in sample.
- The only consistent near-miss is a mild Services demotion (-0.1 z-units): cf +0.00022, z 1.0. It is not
  submitted. If a later pool is larger, it is the one to re-test.
