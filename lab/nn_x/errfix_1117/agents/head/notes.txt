HEAD-OF-LIST ERROR ANALYSIS (ranks 1-10), FD16P20, discovery half of validation, 2026-11-17
Analyst lens: when is rank 1 wrong, what distinguishes a positive at ranks 2-10 from the items above it,
score gaps, the two branches s_ft / s_to, re-weighting inside the head or by feed type.
Scripts: work/a01..a33*.py, helpers work/hk.py (numba NDCG, checked equal to kit.ndcg_per_feed to 3e-16)
and work/clogit.py (within-feed conditional logit). CPU only, 12 threads.

RESULT: ZERO RULES SUBMITTED. No head correction reaches a cross-fitted gain of +0.0003 with z >= 2.
Roughly 1,250 parameter settings in about 20 rule families were tried.

BASELINE AND SIZE OF THE OPPORTUNITY
- Discovery NDCG 0.39939 (7,214 feeds, 697k rows, 11,026 positives).
- Positive rate by rank: 1: 12.6% | 2: 9.1% | 3: 7.4% | 4-5: 6.0% | 6-10: 4.5% | 11-20: 3.0%.
- Best positive at rank 1 in 12.6% of feeds; 2-3: 15.1%; 4-10: 26.3%; 11-30: 26.1%; 31+: 20.0%.
  Share of lost NDCG: rank 2-3 feeds 11%, 4-10 28%, 11-30 33%, 31+ 27%.
- Oracle re-order of the head with the head set fixed: top-2 +0.027, top-3 +0.056, top-5 +0.110,
  top-10 +0.212. The opportunity is large, but none of the columns can find it (below).

1. CAN A FLEXIBLE MODEL RE-RANK THE HEAD? (an upper bound for any rule from these columns)
- LightGBM re-ranker of the top K (all 41 item columns, 8 feed columns, score/s_ft/s_to/rank/gaps),
  cross-fitted over kit.folds:
  binary K=5/10/20: -0.0022/-0.0023/-0.0023 (z about -2.5); lambdarank K=10: -0.0052 (z -3.5).
- Residual GBM (init = per-fold logistic on score, log M, log rank), K=10: 50 rounds -0.00004,
  150 rounds -0.0006, 400 rounds -0.0016; K=3 and K=20 are also negative. Adding rounds only adds loss.
- Ridge conditional logit (score not penalised plus all 46 columns), cross-fitted, K=10 and K=30:
  -0.0011 to -0.0030 at every penalty.
=> With these columns, the head order is at the noise floor. Any perturbation, linear or not, loses
   out of fold. Clicks inside the head are decided by things not in the table (photo, price, title).

2. SWAPS INSIDE THE HEAD
- Rank-1 <-> rank-2 swap gain by quintile of 35 features (rank-1 value and the pair difference),
  308 bins: there is NO bin in which y2 > y1. The best bin is still -0.0007 per feed. Rank 1 beats rank 2
  everywhere, even in the smallest-gap quintile (gap_next < .057: y1 9.8% vs y2 8.4%).
- Cross-fitted regression trees on the swap gain (depth 2-4, leaf 200-800, item/pair/feed features)
  for swaps 1<->2, 2<->3 and 1<->3: all 27 settings are <= 0 out of fold (the worst is -0.0034).
- Confident tops (gap_next > .6, 789 feeds): y1 = 23.8%, and positives differ from negatives mainly by
  M (smaller feeds). No item-level signal.

3. TWO BRANCHES, s_ft vs s_to
- corr(s_ft, s_to) = 0.991. Global weight on s_ft: 0: -0.00113 | .25: -0.00072 | .4: -0.00014 |
  .5: 0 | .6: +0.00001 | .75: -0.00048 | 1: -0.00068. 0.5 is already optimal.
- Head-only re-weighting (top K re-ordered by w*s_ft + (1-w)*s_to, K = 2..300, w = 0..0.6): every
  variant <= +0.00001. Moving toward s_to inside the head costs 0.0005-0.0011.
- When the branches disagree on top-1 (1,378 feeds): ft-top1 is positive 8.6%, to-top1 7.5%,
  score-top1 9.0%. The consensus wins. By segment, H <= 150 looked like it favoured ft
  (0.115 vs 0.060), but this does not survive cross-fitting.
- Head discordant pairs: pairwise accuracy ft .6106, to .6142, score .6140. A pairwise logit favours
  s_to (t ~ .15*dft + .78*dto), but this is pair-count weighted and does not carry over to NDCG
  (head re-weighting toward s_to loses).
- Branch weight per segment, cross-fitted (w chosen per segment on 4 folds; segmentations M, H,
  newest_type, n_hist_cats32, n_feed_cats, newest_src, guest): +0.00007, +0.00014, -0.00012, -0.00002,
  -0.00009, -0.00062, -0.00028. In-sample the best choices gave +0.0002 to +0.0005, all fold noise.
- Disagreement as uncertainty: score - lam*|s_ft - s_to| on the full list: lam .15 +0.00001,
  .3 +0.00010 (z .3), .6 -0.0005. A bonus for disagreement is clearly negative (-0.0008, z -2.7).
  Rank-average of the branches -0.00023. Agreement product +-0.05/0.1: |delta| <= 0.00013.
  Head-local re-standardisation of the branches: K=10 +0.00015 (z 1.2), other K negative.
  Splitting validation by weekday or by fidx quintile shows no drift in the branch balance.

4. SINGLE-FEATURE CORRECTIONS IN THE HEAD
- Within-feed conditional logit (score + one feature), K=10, 42 features: the largest is
  cat_n_in_feed z=+2.9, then pre2_fe_z +2.4, cand_maxcos +2.2, cat_views +2.2 (null max about 2.9).
  K=4: f2 -3.5, f2_home -2.7, c_fav -2.3.
  Category dummies: max |z| 2.5 over about 30 categories x 4 depths. Calibration is fine.
- Tie-breaker scan score + w*z(x), w in {+-.02, +-.04, +-.08, 0}, 41 features, cross-fitted, on the head
  (K=10) and on the full list (574 variants). Best: h10 with w=-.02 (all 5 folds choose it):
  head +0.00042 (z 1.57), full +0.00052 (z 1.95). Next is cat_user_rank (+.02): +0.00034 (z 1.48).
  Every other feature is <= +0.0002 or negative. All five history-similarity columns (h5, h10, h32,
  hmax, hmean5) gain a little at w=-.01 (+0.0002 to +0.0004 in-sample), but w=-.03 to -.05 already
  turns negative, and the conditional-logit sign for h10 is POSITIVE (+0.03). Given about 600 variants
  this is consistent with noise, and it fails the z>=2 bar. Not submitted.
- Category share of the feed (cat_n_in_feed/M): the full-list conditional logit gives z=+6.6, yet
  NDCG for w = .02..0.3 is -0.00007 to -0.0018. The likelihood gain sits in the middle/tail, which
  NDCG barely weights.
- Residual tables against a (rank, M-bucket) calibration show strong-looking bins (hmean5 top decile
  +0.020, z=6.2; gap_top >= 1.4 at -0.017, z=-7). These are FEED-LEVEL confounds (focused-session
  feeds have more positives in the head). The within-feed conditional logit removes them
  (hmean5 z=1.9).

5. INTERACTIONS WITH FEED SEGMENTS (conditional logit, 360 tests per depth)
- K=10: max interaction z 3.1 (cat_n_in_feed x M<=30). K=30: hmax x n_hist_cats32<=2 at z=4.1,
  plus f1, h5, cat_actions, cand_maxcos and cat_n_in_feed x focused user (z 2.5-3.3). This is a coherent
  "focused users deserve sharper affinity" story.
  As NDCG rules, cross-fitted (T in {1,2,3}, w in 0..0.3, 7 features): hmax -0.00003, h5 -0.00019,
  hmean5 -0.00039, f1 -0.00027, cat_actions -0.00011, cat_n -0.00021.
  Small feeds M <= T (T in {20,30,50}): cat_n -0.00001, hmax +0.00047 (z 1.67), f1 -0.00036, hmean5 -0.00002.

6. NEAR-COPIES AND DUPLICATES IN THE HEAD
- Click-splitting hypothesis (two near-identical candidates both in the top 20 share one click):
  REJECTED. 5,212 fingerprinted near-copy pairs in 1,378 feeds: the lower copy is positive 3.65% vs
  3.27% expected, and P(lower pos | upper pos) = 6.2% vs 3.5%. Users open both. Demoting the lower copy
  by 0.05-0.4 gives -0.00002 to -0.00017.
- Near-identical to a history item (>= .995) is 1.3-1.7x the rank-calibrated rate inside the head
  (hmax: 95 positives vs 72 expected) and 0.8x below the head. Promoting it in the head, cross-fitted:
  hmax -0.00015, c_contact -0.00036. The excess is a feed-level effect again.

7. POPULARITY / ENGAGEMENT (other users)
- Very popular items (it_hist_et1_luo 300-800) are positive 1.29x the rank-calibrated rate, but only on
  135 positives. score + w*1[et1 >= T]: in-sample best +0.00048 (T=300, w=.3), cross-fitted +0.00024
  to +0.00048 (z 0.7-1.6). The hinge-log form gives cf +0.00034 (z 1.5). This is a popularity-only rule
  and fails the bar. Not submitted.
- f5 (engagement rate) for well-exposed items (xp_imp_c50 >= 3.9): conditional logit z 1.8-2.4;
  NDCG cross-fitted +0.00024 (z 1.3). Smoothed CTR (Beta prior) z <= 1.3.

8. MISC
- Row order inside a feed is random (rel. position vs label flat, corr with score -0.0008): no leak.
- One tie in the base score in 697k rows, with no positive in it. Tie-breaking cannot help.
- Validation covers two weekdays (1, 3) with no branch drift.

WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE LEAD
- Treat the head of FD16P20 as efficient with respect to every column in this table. Flexible and
  linear re-rankers both lose out of fold, and every swap bin of ranks 1-2 favours the model's order.
  Further head gains need NEW information (images, price, title text, the user's own non-click
  impressions), not re-combination of these columns.
- Do not chase likelihood-based residuals (conditional-logit or calibration z-scores). In this table
  they come from the middle/tail or from feed-level confounds and do not move NDCG.
- The branch balance of 0.5/0.5 is right overall and in every segment we can measure (cross-fitted).
