SYNTHESIS: IS THE PROBLEM NOISE, AND CAN IT BE REMOVED?
Probes: <repo>/lab/nn_x/w12/noise/synthesis/ (scripts, out/). Q = quantify/out/*.txt; F, D = docs lines.

Short answer: noise explains why every team sits near 0.40. It does not explain our 0.003 gap to the leader. The test labels cannot be cleaned, and the training-label noise that can be cleaned is mostly removed already.

1. WHICH NOISE, HOW BIG
- Click draw in val/test labels:
  - It makes up 67% of per-feed NDCG variance.
  - Val scores 0.40294. Labels drawn from our own calibrated softmax would give 0.40137 (+0.00157 ± 0.00175, Q).
  - Draw-only score SE: val 0.0017, public 0.0021, private 0.0014.
- Hidden display position in test (new; 23,460 clean holdout feeds; a student that never saw them):
  - An oracle that knows display slots gains +0.127 NDCG (0.320 -> 0.447).
  - +0.126 of that is the last displayed item. Logs end at a click: 28% of last items are clicked, against 0.93% of other items, and they hold 16% of clicks. That is the label showing through, not removable noise.
  - Slot examination beyond that is worth +0.00084 ± 0.00026, even to an oracle.
- Click draw in training labels: perfect soft labels would add about +0.0011 per member over the current teacher weight of 0.5, and +0.0001 to +0.0008 per file (Q e_bound).
- Position bias in production train clicks:
  - It moves 8.2% of click mass (Q).
  - The top production slot holds 4.75% of non-last click mass, against 2.57% under the member (p_oracle).
  - Its value in NDCG has never been measured on the real network.
- Input drift (lagged popularity counts in test): popularity-light views gave +0.000523 and +0.00032 on public (F513, F516). Stale-view fixes were null or worse (F525, F529).
- Evaluation noise:
  - Near-copy files differ almost purely by the label draw (Q).
  - The smallest pool effect we can detect is 0.00086 on val and 0.00112 on public (D04:137).
  - Choosing the best of several files inflates its score by 0.00045-0.00087 (D04:185).

2. WHAT LIMITS THE TEST SCORE
- The level: the test click draw, truncation included. Every team shares it.
- The gap:
  - The leader's +0.00305 equals a model 2.5% sharper, about 0.012-0.016 nats per click.
  - That equals our whole member-to-file step (0.017 nats): information, not cleaning.
  - The gap's z is 2.1-3.6, so it is real.
- Learning only: the training draw and position bias, at most about +0.001 val together.
- Decisions only: evaluation noise.

3. WHAT ALREADY REMOVED NOISE
- Paid:
  - Distillation: +0.00257 per member; +0.001032 public.
  - Equal feed weights: +0.0020 / +0.0006 (F434).
  - Production-order auxiliary loss gated by 1 - p_random: +0.0024 / +0.0027 (F436).
  - 2% random-order cooldown: +0.00117 / +0.00134 (F402).
  - Popularity-light views.
- Failed:
  - Label smoothing; down-weighting surprising clicks (-0.00058); OHEM/DRO; cooldown on selected data (-0.0035); cross-fitted teacher; pseudo-labels; cascade truncation (+0.00004).
  - No random-click share was found that a robust loss could remove (Q).
- Correction to the inventory: the short-feed p_random fix was already read in cooldown (val +0.00025 ± 0.00035, holdout -0.00019; its members added less than seed duplicates; status-log 732). That lever is closed; only a from-scratch run was not done.

4. REMAINING CANDIDATES
(a) Teacher scored with the random-order domain row (the row val/test use) instead of the production row.
- Probe (p_token.py, one student, 3,000 seen production feeds):
  - The row switch moves per-feed softmax TV 0.045, against 0.037 on random-order feeds.
  - It cuts top-slot mass by 12.5%.
  - It shifts slot-bin mass by 0.009, against a 0.073 gap between clicks and the model.
- Never run. 7 of 11 teacher members lack checkpoints, so add the row shift of the 4 w5_e10_base members to the stored teacher.
- Cost: 8 dumps (~15 GPU-min) plus a 16-student cooldown re-run (~1 h).
- Expected: -0.0002 to +0.0004 val.
(b) Click half IPS-weighted on production feeds, with 1/e(slot) from the clean random-order pool. Slot-0 e is 1.97, against 2.15 in the old pool (p_curve).
- Only the CPU MLP proxy ran, without the auxiliary loss or domain rows. It read -0.00017 ± 0.00025 per member and -0.0014 on long feeds.
- Cost: ~1 h; it can share a control with (a).
- Expected: -0.0005 to +0.0003.
(c) From-scratch --prand2 run, only on idle GPUs. Expected: -0.0005 to +0.0003.
Read rule (declare before running): promote if the 16-pool val difference against same-root control re-cools is >= +0.0005, with randomization p < 0.05 and the private-mix view >= 0. Kill if val < 0. The holdout is reported but cannot veto.
Not worth running: truncated/GCE losses, confident learning, co-teaching, label smoothing, a higher teacher weight, input rescaling (6 of 94 constructions at z > 2 against 2.1 by chance, F471), test-time augmentation.

5. BOTTOM LINE
The labels are very noisy, but the val/test noise is shared and cannot be removed. Positions would only reveal the click, and slot examination is worth +0.0008 even to an oracle. Distillation already took most of the training-side noise. The remainder, about +0.001 val at best, is below the 0.0032 gap. Running (a) and (b) together (~2 h on the GPUs) closes the question, but expect about zero. Closing the gap needs more information in the inputs. Do not choose files on public differences under 0.0005-0.0008.
