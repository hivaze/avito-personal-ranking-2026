IMPLEMENTATION NOTE (quantile balancer, design definition kept): theta = the (k+1)-th largest biased score. The shift q is exact for an
  OVERLOADED expert (b -= q alone gives it exactly k/E on the batch) but only partial for an UNDERLOADED one (the margin of a non-selected
  token is measured to v_(k+1), not to the k-th score it must beat; tests/test_moe_cpu.py T2). In the synthetic low-diversity drifting stream
  it still takes the load entropy from 0.33 / 0.50 to >= 0.95 within 50 steps (eta 0.1).
