    Mechanism: a GBDT over 3.1M train feeds with an OUT-OF-FOLD NN score as its main feature learns the residual the NN leaves

      pool feeds excluded from every fold's training), 10 epochs each: 5 x 4.5 GPU-h = 22 GPU-h (~3-4 h wall on 6-the GPUs);
      the 5 fold models also give a 5-member TO pool for free. (2) Score every train feed with its fold model (OOF), val/test
      with the 5-fold mean. (3) LightGBM / CatBoost YetiRank on a 500K-1M-feed train subsample + val folds (user-grouped, OOF)

    Expected gain: member 0..+0.002 val (P ~0.15 for >= +0.001); pool +0.0003..+0.0015 if the GBDT reaches >= 0.398 val at
      rho <= 0.9 to G3 (break-even measured at ~0.393 val for rho 0.856, cb26).

    Kill: GBDT val OOF < best single TO member + 0.0005 (0.3967 + 0.0005), or holdout < 0 vs that member, or pool delta on G3

    Mechanism: the pool is correlation-limited (rho 0.93, n_eff 1.07); a member with a different mean function (history filtered

      trained with the same debiasing aux) at >= 0.393 val and rho <= 0.9 is worth +0.0003..+0.0008 at pool level (frontier law).
    Protocol: nn/model.py variant flags (--hist_filter target --hist_cap 50 --no_dense --head dcn --loss listmle), 2 seeds x
      10 epochs (9 GPU-h) then 6 seeds if the pair passes; judged as a family (gates a+b) and as an addition to G3.
    Expected: pool +0.0003..+0.0008 (P ~0.15). Kill: single val < 0.393 or rho to G3 > 0.92 or G3+family <= 0 on val and holdout.
D4  TRUNC chain (running): E 10-epoch pairs read ~23:00, trunc2 ~00:10, replication seeds 955/956 ~01:00; pre-declared rules
    stand. Pool +0.0002..+0.0005 (P ~0.3). Nothing to add from this audit except: an E FAIL driven by M<50 does not close TRUNC
    (per-M lifts in trunc2).
D5  BOARD WATCH + SELECTION DISCIPLINE (0 GPU). Snapshot the full board daily (teams, entries, last) to resolve H1/H7 (a merge
    = vanished rows + summed entries; a reference = never submits again); never compare against another team's entry at better
    than +-0.0015; freeze the Dec-9 protocol (S = min(V, V-D), pmix / stale vetoes, pick 2 = least test-correlated eligible pool).
Not proposed (closed with numbers): ID collaborative channels (altapproach), pretrained-encoder init (R3 stage 0 FAIL 22:00),
age offsets (AGE-PROD FAIL, post-hoc +0.0003), w_ft / M-split / H-split routing, calendar priors (H4), LLM backbones,
architecture blocks (r7 0/13), more same-recipe seeds (asymptote +0.0002), exploration-only training (0.384 / 0.382).
Honest bottom line: no design above has an expected gain near +0.005; D2 is the only one whose published precedent is of that
order, and its prior here is ~0.15. If #1 is H2, the most likely reason we cannot see the lever is screen power, not a missing
idea; the 2-day budget is best spent on D2 + D1 + the TRUNC chain, with D3 only on idle lanes.
