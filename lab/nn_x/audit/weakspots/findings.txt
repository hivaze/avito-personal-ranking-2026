    Every M<50 claim rests on the same 5,893 val feeds that every nested rule has already used.
3.2 Seed/checkpoint-level selection is inside noise (1.3). Positive: "keep last checkpoint" (wave-5 hygiene) costs nothing.
3.3 Screen power: single-run val sd 0.0008-0.0011 and same-seed non-determinism |dval| 0.0007 give the 2-seed 2-epoch
    screens an MDE of ~0.0010-0.0016, while every lever in INSIGHTS expects +0.0003-0.0008. The registry's CLOSED list is
    therefore "not large at screen power", not "null"; e.g. r7's 13 blocks, Q2 typed/adrop0/a4w. Do not reopen them, but
    do not cite them as proof when a mechanism recurs.
3.4 Val over-use: public = 0.0022 + 0.976 val over 8 points; both val-best pools under-performed on public; three nested
    +2 SE combiner effects (M-split, S2, raw pooling) failed member-level replication within 24 h. The public LB paired
    SE 0.0005-0.0007 cannot adjudicate anything below ~0.0015, so public cannot rescue val.
3.5 The holdout rewards popularity/memorisation inputs 2.5x (review_leakage LEAKAGE-1) and is the population furthest
    from test in time; it is still the best FAMILY-level lens (rho 0.93) and ordered the 5 public pools better than val
    (Spearman +0.70 vs +0.10, n=5, context only).
3.6 user0 (4.6% of val, 4.1% test): the FT half is +0.0049+-0.0030 there (T7) - fine, but any user-level feature or fold
    logic treats it as a bucket; presub reports the slice.
3.7 Public is 100% weekend, private 24%, val 0%: the only weekend evidence is the train holdout (m02 gain larger on
    weekend feeds). No lens can show a weekday-trained artefact on public; accepted risk.
3.8 Per-feed z-mean at small M: measured, insensitive (1.4).
3.9 FT fold-bagging asymmetry on test: measured, negligible at pool level (1.2).
