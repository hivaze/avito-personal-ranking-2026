0. VERDICT IN SIX LINES
=====================================================================================================================
1. Every headline number reproduces (val/holdout of all 7 pools to 4e-6; k_v 0.14 ± 0.34; holdout->public chi2 0.27/4;
   half-split P 0.011-0.018; PF7x > Pall on val/holdout/public). The picks (G3; PF7x as pick 2) are the best-supported ones and
   this critique does not change them. What it changes is how much the evidence supports the STORY and the RULE.
2. The report overstates the rejection of 1:1 transfer: the quoted "LR p 0.002-0.005" ignores the covariance of the 6 contrasts
   through G3's own public draw (error correlations 0.13-0.85). With the full covariance 1:1 is an acceptable model by goodness
   of fit (chi2 8.2/6, p 0.22), k=1 is 2.5 SE from the fit (LR p 0.013), and "1:1 + a common G3 offset" is accepted at p 0.27.
   Dropping the two val-selected pools (M2, S2) leaves k_v = 0.35 ± 0.41: 1:1 is then 1.6 SE away, i.e. not rejected. The
   "val does not transfer" finding and the "val selection explains it" finding are the SAME two data points read twice.
3. The holdout is not "selection-free". Every member's checkpoint was selected on it (select_on: 'holdout' in every family's
   training args; CURRENT_STATUS 34, 281), and G3 itself was built at 13:55 on 11-12 as "the holdout-best" of the decide4
   interim pools. The report applies winner's-curse arithmetic to the val maxima (M2, S2) but not to the holdout maximum (G3),
   whose holdout edge over M2 / PF7x (+0.00018 ± 0.00019 / +0.00020 ± 0.00022) is inside that margin (+0.0002..+0.0003 for K 5-8).
   The mechanism in calib B3 ("m02 members whose checkpoints were picked on val") is refuted; what survives is pool-level selection.
4. "The holdout is the lens that transfers" is one data point (M2) better than "0.3 x val": chi2 0.27 vs 1.5 on 4 df (both
   accepted; zero gives 1.75); LOO sum z^2 0.18 vs 1.84, of which 1.3 is M2; without M2 the two are indistinguishable (0.17 vs
   0.56 on 3 df; k_v on Cd/Pall/PF7x 0.39 ± 0.41) and the absolute-level LOO rmse is 0.00019 vs 0.00029. Kendall tau with
   public over the 5 TO pools is 0.60 vs 0.20, P(tau >= 0.6 | random) 0.12; only 2 of the 10 pairs are >= 1 SE from 0 on public.
5. The pre-registered rule has NO demonstrated out-of-sample skill. In the LOO over the 7 scored pools 7 of 8 folds are
   uninformative (G3 present -> every holdout-led rule picks G3, which is public-best); in the one informative fold (G3 held out)
   the rule's E = H + 0.15 V' picks M2 (public rank 4 of 6, -0.00029 vs the public-best PF7x), because the holdout ties M2 and
   PF7x (+0.00002 ± 0.00027) and the val tie-break goes the way public contradicted. Its verification clause (2.5 SE) can only
   fire on |P - H| >= 0.0012-0.0017, 5-25x the observed disagreements; its switch bar (~+0.0005 on the holdout) is ~10x the
   spread of every near-copy built so far. The rule is "G3 stays", which is defensible, but it is a freeze, not a validated rule.
6. Several secondary numbers are presented with more precision than they carry: the combined private estimate for TOonly
   (-0.00001 ± 0.00010) is an artefact of giving an uninformative lens (holdout d = 0 ± 0 by construction) 90% of the weight;
   the P(beats G3) probabilities carry no model or selection term; the "6 later files missed by -0.00125 on average" is one
   independent reading, not six; the M2 holdout weekday/weekend split is cited in two opposite directions by two analyses.

Not recomputed (taken as read): the val-vs-test construction tables (semantics 1), the hour/outage analysis (semantics 2C), the
label-free test-side disagreement diagnostics (landscape 4), the slice tables (landscape 2c), the FT-half pos_cold decomposition.

=====================================================================================================================
5. OVERFITTING TO 7 POINTS: WHERE THE REPORT CROSSES THE LINE
=====================================================================================================================
F1 Six contrasts carry about two independent facts: (i) the public spread (0.0007) is a third of the val spread (0.002); (ii) the two
   pools above G3 on val are below it on public. Every model in section 1.3 (generic shrinkage, val-selected indicator, re-cool
   share, val-holdout discrepancy, holdout 1:1, zero) is fitted to and "accepted" by these two facts. Ranking them by LOO sum z^2
   on 4-6 points (0.7 vs 3.1 vs 3.7 vs 5.9) and declaring a best one is fitting a story to the residuals; the report says
   "differences < 2 are not evidence" and then builds R4 on the winner.
F2 The "val-selected" indicator was defined after the residuals were seen and coincides with the sign of d_val. Any explanation of
   the form "pools above G3 on val regress" is unfalsifiable on these 6 points because G3 is the reference and the two pools above
   it are the two that regressed.
F3 The holdout's "no free parameter, chi2 0.27" is tighter than chance (P 0.05, which the report notes) and is one point (M2) better
   than a 0.3 x val rule. The report upgrades it from "consistent" to "the lens that transfers" and then gives it 85-90% of the
   weight in the private estimate and 100% of the switch decision in R4.
F4 The rule's constants (0.15 = fitted k_v; 2 SE_E; 2.5 SE verification; 0.3 / 0.6 shrink in semantics) are set from the same 6
   points; the LOO shows no out-of-sample skill (0/1 informative folds) and the 7/8 headline is G3 seven times.
F5 The "pre-registered predictions for unscored pools" (landscape 6c) use H3 = a + b x pubM fitted with 2 parameters on 6 points; its
   prediction is "-0.00037 for everyone" (the mean residual), and its intercept IS G3's own public draw. A PoP3 or F10only reading
   near -0.0004 is therefore equally consistent with "1:1 transfer + G3 lucky by 0.0004" (accepted at p 0.27, N1) as with shrinkage:
   the probes cannot separate H1-with-offset from H3, so their advertised 2.2 / 1.8 SE readability is between H3 and the WRONG null.
F6 Private statements ("composition cannot re-order the pools by more than ~0.0005", P(beats G3), "private = public + 0.0026") are
   derived from val slices and the holdout, which the report itself says are blind to private's cold >= 0.45 tail (33%) and to M<50
   feeds (39%, holdout) or weekends (val). "± 0.0005" is a statement about the resolution of the lenses, not about private.
