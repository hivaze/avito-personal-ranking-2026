=====================================================================================================================================
5. INTERPRETATION: WHAT VAL MEASURES, WHAT WE MAY HAVE BEEN MISREADING
=====================================================================================================================================
5a. Val's level and its composition-driven slices are reliable: M-reweighted val predicts public to +0.0005 +- 0.0008 for every
    modern file, the old files to +-0.0016, and the label-free marginals of val / public / private match except hour, cold share and
    weekend (section 2b, reports/eda_shift_overlap.md). Nothing here suggests val is the wrong population.
5b. Val's between-pool deltas among near-copies (within-feed corr >= 0.995, |d| <= 0.002) are not reliable as point estimates:
    they transferred to public at 0.05-0.14 (CI up to 0.67) and are rejected at 1:1 (p 0.003-0.005). Three mechanisms of about the
    right size are visible and not separable: val-selection optimism (0.0005-0.0008 for the val-best of 10-50 pools), a Tue-heavy
    component of G3-type pools' val edge (+0.0005..+0.0017 Thu-Tue, <= 1.7 SE), and the fact that val-period re-cooled / m02 trunks
    (70-100% of G3 / M2 / S2 / TOonly) gain on val what the train-period holdout sees at 1/3 strength. Public is not "noisier than
    expected" (the noise model held: residuals chi2 4.8 / 3 on the first 3 points, nn_x/pubdiag/noise); it is that the val
    differences were mostly not there to transfer.
5c. The one val component we priced at zero is the one with the clearest forward signal: the FT half (val-label fine-tune, 0.5
    weight). Inside every pool its val value is 0 +- 0.0004 because it trades warm positives (-0.0017) for cold positives (+0.0051
    on all-cold-positive feeds); public read +0.00047 (z 0.9) for keeping it; private is colder (privMC +0.0006 for keeping it, 3c);
    label-free it is the component that promotes cold candidates (4c). This does not license changing w_ft (the val curve is flat
    within +-0.0005, the holdout cannot evaluate FT, and w_ft was closed three times, critique2 R6); it does say that the FT half is
    not the part of G3 at risk on private.
5d. Does a better solution follow from re-reading val? Not a new pool: every candidate we can build is within 0.0007 of G3 on val,
    within 0.0003 on the holdout and within 0.0007 on public, and label-free they are the same ranking on private (4a-4c). The
    reinterpretation changes the DECISION RULE, not the candidates: val should gate (leak/overfit checks, level, slice vetoes), and
    the ranking among gated near-copies should come from the samples that are not val-selected and matched public - the holdout
    and the public readout itself (section 6). It also closes the door on re-litigating M2 / S2 / U3-type candidates on val alone.
5e. What would change this picture: a public reading of a pool that is >= 0.001 above G3 on val AND >= +1 SE on the holdout (none
    exists; U1/U3 tie G3 on the holdout), or two of the pre-registered probes in 6c landing on the 1:1 predictions.

=====================================================================================================================================
7. LIMITS OF THIS ANALYSIS
=====================================================================================================================================
- 7 modern public points, 6 contrasts, SE_tot 0.0004-0.0008 each, all sharing G3's own public draw: a 1:1-vs-shrinkage distinction
  at p ~0.005 is the only inferential result; the transfer factor itself is known to +-0.3; H0 / H3 / H5 are not distinguishable.
- Composition reweighting (pubM / pubMC / post-stratification) assumes the within-type val delta is the within-type test delta; it
  cannot see a mechanism that is val-period-specific inside a type (which is exactly what the shortfalls suggest).
- The holdout is train-period, TO-only, production-traffic-like (random-like filter), and its by-day contrasts swing +-0.0015: it
  is the right sample for "no val labels", not for "the test period". Its agreement with public (chi2 1.2 / 6) is one sample's worth.
- The label-defined slices (npos, pos_cold, g3_pos_rank) cannot be weighted to test; the pos_cold counterfactual uses p3's
  label-free estimate of the test cold-positive mix.
- S1 was rebuilt as 0.5 z(ft) + 0.5 z(to) of the stored hfit arrays (val 0.40046); the submitted cand_S1 file may weight the halves
  differently (its recorded val, +0.00054 vs member-equal PF7x, is not reproduced exactly). PoP3's member list is the union (52) and
  its re-cool share is nominal. Old generations: test arrays are exact (from the submission files); val arrays were rebuilt from the
  recorded recipes for v10 / v5 only (match to 2e-5); v3 / v4 use recorded val numbers.
