VAL SEMANTICS VS TEST: what val is, how it relates to the public score, and how val should be used for selection
nn_x/audit/valpub/semantics (2026-11-13, CPU only, 12 threads, new files only; scripts lib.py + s01..s06, logs in out/)
Conventions: +- = paired per-feed SE over the 14,423 val feeds unless stated. G3 = cand_G_F10F7xF7x70_memeq (val 0.40032, public
0.392820). "Modern 7" = Cd, Pall, PF7x, G3, M2, S2, TOonly (exact public scores on record; val/test arrays reproduced in lib.py to the
documented val numbers, out/pf_*.npy). Public = test ts < 1790497920 (8,562 feeds) as everywhere in the project. Holdout = the 30K
train-period random-like M>=50 feeds scored by train-only pools (nn_x/pubdiag/shift/shiftlib.holdout_index). MSK = UTC+3.

=====================================================================================================================
0. BOTTOM LINE (ten lines)
=====================================================================================================================
1. Val and test are the SAME construction. Every intrinsic descriptor (M, H, user-0 share, train-user share, category mix, f1..f5,
   listing age from f4, history composition) agrees between val and test to within the val Tue-vs-Thu yardstick; JS(cat) val-vs-test
   0.0002 (Tue-vs-Thu 0.0006). The only differences are (a) staleness of train-derived item statistics (cold share 0.29 -> 0.40,
   rising 2 pp/day) and (b) the hour histogram, which is mostly outages plus the public/private boundary cut (s01 1A/1B, s02 2C).
2. The hour-of-day story is largely an artefact. Val lost Tue 20h and Thu 20-21h to outages; with outage hours filled, the 14-24h
   share is val 0.517, Sat 0.538, Sun 0.554, Mon 0.516, Tue 0.533 (raw: val 0.482, private 0.597). Hour has no effect on
   positives-per-feed or on NDCG once M is controlled (all hour coefficients within 1 SE; s01 1D), and no hour term reaches |t| 2
   in any pool-delta model except h06-09 for M2/S2 (-0.0008..-0.0012, among 105 tests; s02 2D). Hour reweighting does not improve val->public
   calibration (s04 A/B). Drop the hour reprojection machinery; keep the M-mix for the level.
3. Positives per feed do not depend on hour or day given M (npos ~ logM +0.29, Thu -0.02 +- 0.03, hour terms -0.02..-0.07 +- 0.06).
   The NDCG level by hour is an M effect (random-ranking baseline tracks it exactly). The >=1-positive selection makes the LEVEL
   depend on P(npos | M) only; it does not touch within-feed ranking. The level is predictable from M alone: public = M-mix(val)
   + 0.0005 +- 0.0008 for the modern generation (s04 A). The feared extra staleness penalty of -0.001..-0.002 (review_shift) has
   not shown up on public, which already has 0.735 of feeds at cold share > 0.3 (private 0.836).
4. Public vs private differ beyond cold only in the hour cut (SMD +0.53, by construction) and in nothing else (|SMD| < 0.10 for
   30 other descriptors; s02 2B). On the holdout, public-like (Sat + Sun morning) vs private-like (Sun afternoon - Tue) pool
   contrasts differ by +0.0007 +- 0.0007 at most (s02 2E). Projected public->private pool-delta shifts from the val regressions are
   +0.0003..+0.0007 +- 0.0003 (all pools gain slightly vs G3 on colder / evening feeds; 1.1-2.4 SE, 105 tests; s02 2D). Nothing in
   composition can re-order the pools between public and private by more than ~0.0005.
5. THE CORE FINDING: within the modern generation val differences did not transfer to public. Regressing the 6 exact public deltas
   vs G3 on the val deltas gives slope b = +0.10 +- 0.32 (raw; 0.05-0.22 under every reweighting), chi2(b = 1) 10.9/6, Kendall tau of
   the 7-pool orderings 0.05 (p 0.5). The 7 public scores are MORE alike than sampling noise predicts (sd 0.00024 vs expected SE
   0.00036, chi2 3.1/6) while on val they differ far beyond noise (chi2 49/6). Random half-splits of val give b = 0.79 +- 0.42 and
   P(b <= 0.10) = 0.015: the non-transfer is not sampling noise (s04 B, s05 A/B).
6. The pattern is a val-selection / val-fitting excess, not a composition effect: pools ranked ABOVE G3 on val (M2, S2; both
   val-selected) came in 0.0009-0.0010 BELOW their val prediction, pools below G3 on val (Cd, Pall, PF7x) came in 0.0005-0.0010
   ABOVE it (s04 B2). The selection-free holdout shrinks the val-TO ordering the same way (slope 0.36 +- 0.31; s05 C), and the
   expected winner's-curse excess of a val max over K near-copies is +0.0005..+0.0009 for K = 5..50 (s04 D), the size observed. The
   re-cool-share story of critique2 is collinear with this (corr 0.96) and not separable on these pools; the practical rule is the same.
7. Between generations val transfers 1:1 (public = 0.0071 + 0.964 val over 11 points incl. v3/v4/v5; resid sd 0.0008): val is a
   good proxy for changes >= 0.002 and a poor proxy for near-copy differences < 0.0015 at corr > 0.99 (s04 A).
8. The val/test candidate SET is ranker-shaped while the display order is random: positives sit at f1 percentile 0.38 (random 0.50),
   f1 alone scores 0.336 vs random 0.290. In train the production order is only weakly a function of f1 (within-feed Spearman 0.09),
   so "production-order information" is not f1; it lives in the position tower / K9 aux, which val/test cannot evaluate. f1's value
   is identical Tue vs Thu (-0.0026 +- 0.0035) and flat over the 12 train days on the holdout (trend -0.00015 +- 0.00023 per day;
   s03 3A/3B, s06 6A). There is no measurable drift of pool agreement within test beyond what cold share explains (s03 3C).
9. The cross-feed "later history" channel exists in test exactly as in val (103 of 108 same-user pairs carry the earlier feed's
   items; row rate 1.48% vs 1.30% on val) but the overlay moved public by 0.000000: those feeds are not scored or have no positives
   (CURRENT_STATUS 21:52; s01 1E). The FT half did not learn the leak (FT-TO on the 117 val leak feeds -0.0024 +- 0.0050; s06 6B).
10. Pre-registered rule (section 6): use val for level and for generational changes; treat near-copy val deltas as 0.3 x val unless
   replicated on the holdout (TO halves) and on both val days; never rank near-copies on public (public can only reject differences
   >= 0.0015); subtract the winner's-curse term when the candidate is a val max over K pools; drop hour / Tue-Thu / age / user-mix
   reweighting; keep M-mix (level) and M x cold (veto only).

   0.015; P(chi2 >= 10.85) = 0.11. The time split Tue -> Thu gives b = 0.67 +- 0.35. The selection-free holdout gives slope 0.36 +-
   0.31 on the val-TO deltas of Cd/Pall/PF7x/M2 vs G3 (chi2(b=1) 4.9/4; M2-G3 +0.00036 val-TO (M >= 50) vs -0.00018 holdout). So the
   val ordering of the modern near-copies is val-specific beyond sampling noise, and the one labelled sample that shares none of
   val's fitting or selection (holdout) sees ~1/3 of it. Winner's-curse arithmetic: the expected val-only excess of the best of K
   near-copies is SE_val x E[max_K] = +0.00045 (K 5), +0.00059 (K 10), +0.00072 (K 20), +0.00087 (K 50) at SE_val 0.00039; the
   observed shortfall of M2/S2 is -0.0009..-0.0010. The numbers match without any re-cool-specific mechanism, and the re-cool
   mechanism (members selected on val inside the cooldown) is itself a form of val selection.

=====================================================================================================================
5. WHAT 7 PUBLIC POINTS CAN AND CANNOT SUPPORT
=====================================================================================================================
CAN: (i) reject 1:1 transfer of val near-copy deltas (b = 1 is 2.2-3.0 SE away under every predictor; the half-split null puts the
   realised b at its 1.5th percentile); (ii) show that the 7 modern pools are indistinguishable on public (chi2 3.1/6); (iii)
   confirm the between-generation 1:1 level transfer and the M-mix offset (+0.0005 +- 0.0008 modern; +0.0012 v10).
CANNOT: (i) choose between "val-selection shrinkage" and "re-cool val-only excess" (corr 0.96); (ii) estimate b more precisely
   than +-0.3, i.e. distinguish b = 0.3 from b = 0; (iii) detect any composition-driven re-ordering (projected shifts 0.0003-0.0007
   with SE 0.0003 need 4-10 more readouts of twins); (iv) say anything about weekend effects (public is 100% weekend, private 24%,
   val 0%); the holdout is the only labelled weekend sample and sees nothing above 2 SE; (v) read any difference < 0.0015 between
   near-copies in one submission (SE 0.00045-0.00080 incl. val).
Pre-specified hypotheses and verdicts: H-hour (hour mix moves pool deltas): REJECTED (s01 1D, s02 2D, s04 4C). H-stale-Thu (the
   staler val day is the better test proxy): NOT SUPPORTED (4C). H-cold (M x cold reweighting improves transfer): WEAK (chi2 10.8 ->
   8.3 / 5.9, b unchanged, n_eff 14.3K -> 9.3K; keep as veto only). H-leak (val leak feeds distort pool comparisons): REJECTED
   (< 0.00003). H-selection (val-selected pools regress on public): SUPPORTED at ~2 SE (4D, 4E). H-composition val/test (val is a
   different population from test): REJECTED (section 1). H-ranker-staleness (production inputs lose value on later days):
   REJECTED (3B, 3E).

=====================================================================================================================
6. PRE-REGISTERED RULE FOR USING VAL IN SELECTION (from now on; stated before any further public readout)
=====================================================================================================================
R1 LEVEL: predict public / private levels with the M-only reweighting (calib.py) plus +0.0005 (modern generation); expect private
   = public + 0.0026 (M mix). Do not use M x cold (pmix) or hour reweighting for levels.
R2 GENERATIONAL changes (val delta >= 0.002, within-feed corr < 0.99 to the reference): val transfers ~1:1; a single val readout at
   >= 3 SE is sufficient, confirmed by the holdout sign.
R3 NEAR-COPY changes (val delta < 0.0015, corr >= 0.99): the expected realised value is 0.3 x val delta (range 0.1-0.4 from s04/s05)
   unless the change is confirmed on the holdout with train-only halves at >= 1 SE in the same direction AND is positive on both val
   days; then use 0.6 x. A near-copy change is adopted only if the shrunk delta exceeds 2 SE_val (SE_val 0.0003-0.0005): unconfirmed
   that needs d_val >= ~0.0026, i.e. in practice a near-copy change is never adopted on val alone; holdout-confirmed it needs d_val
   >= ~0.0013. Below that the change is NEUTRAL and the pick is decided by R8 (holdout and simplicity), not by the val number.
R4 WINNER'S CURSE: when a candidate is the best of K pools evaluated on val for the same decision, subtract SE_val(d) x E[max_K]
   (0.00045 at K 5, 0.0006 at K 10, 0.0007 at K 20, 0.0009 at K 50) from its val delta vs a non-selected reference before applying
   R3. Log K with every decision.
R5 WHAT TO IGNORE: hour-of-day slices and reprojections (no effect given M; the private-hour gap is outages + boundary), Tue-vs-Thu
   as a staleness proxy (use the two days only as a replication check, R3), listing-age / user-mix / category reweightings (no
   shift), the 117 leak feeds (no effect), weekend reasoning from public (unidentifiable).
R6 WHAT TO KEEP: the holdout as a co-equal, selection-free lens for every TO-side change (it shrinks val's near-copy ordering by
   ~2/3, in line with public); the M x cold pmix delta as a VETO only (< -1 SE blocks), never as a promoter; presub_gate.
R7 PUBLIC READOUTS: use them to verify a declared pick once (reject if below the M-mix prediction by > 2 SE_pub + 0.001) and to
   test generational claims; never to rank near-copies (the 7 modern pools are inside noise on public), never with slice twins for
   hour. A re-cool-share "discount" is not identifiable and is not applied (agrees with critique2 R7).
R8 PICK LOGIC under R1-R7: among pools whose val delta vs the current pick, after R3/R4 shrinkage, is within +-2 SE_val, prefer the
   one with the higher holdout TO score and the lower share of val-selected components; the current data put G3, PF7x, M2, S2,
   TOonly inside one such band (holdout 0.32360-0.32379: G3 best, M2 and PF7x tied next; PF7x has the smallest val-selected
   share). This is a rule for future candidates; it is stated here, not fitted to the 7 public numbers (the 7 numbers only say
   that near-copy val differences do not transfer).
