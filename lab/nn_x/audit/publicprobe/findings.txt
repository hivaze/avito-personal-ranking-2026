Scope: a prioritised, pre-registered programme of public submissions whose EXACT public deltas vs G3 (scores shown to 10
digits for the fixed 8,562 public feeds) answer what val cannot: (1) input-family carry-over to the test period, (2) member-

  out/gate_cand_P_*.txt (presub_gate output), out/sha256.txt
Conventions: d = per-feed val NDCG(probe) - NDCG(G3), paired over 14,423 val feeds. "pubmix"/"privmix" = d reweighted to the
public / private feed-size (M) mix (src/calib.py). Public paired SE = sqrt(Var_pubmix(d) / 8,562), private = sqrt(Var_privmix(d)
/ 20,033): the 'Mw' noise model of nn_x/pubdiag/noise, which matched the realised public residuals (chi2(3) p 0.19). "pmix" =
M x cold-share reweighting (src/pmix.py; report-only, level-miscalibrated by +0.004, deltas still informative). G3 = preds/
ens_G3_final_* = z(0.5 z(ft) + 0.5 z(to)) over F10 (6) + F7x (7) + F7x70 (7); rebuilt from the member files at corr 1.000000.
Slice-restricted probe (A on slice S, G3 elsewhere): exact public delta = (1/8562) sum_{i in public & S} d_i; expectation
(n_S_pub/8562) * mean_S(d) with M-reweighting inside S; SE = sqrt(n_S_pub * Var_S(d)) / 8562. Everything is from existing
preds/ arrays; no model was run.

5. COMBINING MANY PUBLIC READOUTS INTO A PRIVATE-ORIENTED FINAL PICK WITHOUT OVERFITTING PUBLIC
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1 What public is for. Public is a labelled sample of the test period (not of val) that is private-like on coverage and M, and
    unlike private on hour (morning-heavy) and weekend (100%). It is used (a) to estimate transfer FACTORS of named components
    (re-cool share, FT branch, M<50 recipe, popularity family), (b) to reproject one or two decisive contrasts by hour, and (c) to
    verify every declared pick once. It is NOT used to rank near-copies: picking the max public score among K near-copies with
    SE 0.0005 inflates by ~0.0005 * sqrt(2 ln K) (~0.001 at K = 20), larger than every real difference we have.
5.2 Frozen candidate list (<= 6; additions only through a pre-declared decide rule, each addition logged here with its date):
    G3 (reference) | N1 = G3+NEW (32) | NEWx = NEW12 + F10 (18; share 0) | Pall (38; share 0, pick-2 hedge) | T1 = G3 + TRUNC
    members if the wave-5 E readout passes | the w_ft / M-split variant of whichever of these wins, as ONE variant only.
    Each candidate gets at most one full public readout (its own file) and at most two slice twins. Hard cap: 30 programme
    submissions in total (Tier A 3, Tier B <= 6, G1 1, candidate readouts <= 6, twins <= 8, reserve 6). Every look is logged.
5.3 Estimator for candidate C vs G3 (all deltas paired, per feed):
      V_C = pmix-private reweighted val delta (src/pmix.py; SE_v = pmix SE)   [val, private composition]
      D_C = -b_hat * (share_C - 0.70)                                          [re-cool discount, b_hat +- SE_b from 4.3]
      P_C = hour-reprojected public delta: 0.403 * d_pub(h<14)/0.639 + 0.597 * d_pub(h>=14)/0.361 when C's h14 twin exists,
            else the full public delta r(C) (SE_p = SE_pub(C), inflated x1.3 when no twin exists, for the composition gap)
      H_C = holdout delta of the train-only pool (SE_h)                        [selection-free lens, eligibility only]
      S_C = 0.5 * (V_C + D_C) + 0.5 * P_C,  SE_S = 0.5 * sqrt(SE_v^2 + (share_C - 0.70)^2 SE_b^2 + SE_p^2)
    (0.5/0.5 is the RESEARCH_SWEEP plan "val shrunk + 0.5 public"; val's SE_v is further multiplied by sqrt(1 + 0.1 * n_looks)
    where n_looks = number of candidates evaluated on val for this decision, as the selection inflation of a val max.)
5.4 Switch and hedge rules: pick 1 = G3 unless some C has S_C > 2 SE_S, H_C >= -1 SE_h, no val slice (M<50, M>=50, Tue, Thu)
    < -2 SE, pmix >= -1 SE and presub_gate PASS; among qualifiers the highest S_C. Pick 2 = the eligible pool (S >= -1 SE_S,
    H >= -1 SE_h) with the LOWEST within-feed test correlation to pick 1 (the hedge; currently Pall at 0.9971 vs G3). If two
    candidates are within 1 SE_S, prefer the lower re-cool share (the component with the demonstrated val-only excess).
5.5 Reading twins into private: a slice twin only ever reprojects a contrast; it never promotes a slice-specific recipe (the
    M-split is the one exception and needs 4.2's 2-SE bar on the exact public slice delta plus the hour twin).
5.6 Timing: Tier A today; B1/B2 (+ conditional twins) over the next 2 days; G1 when a GPU lane is free; candidate readouts only
    after their decide rule exists; the final estimator is run once, on a fixed date (Dec 7), with every number already on
    record, and the two picks are the output of that single run.

6. WHAT PUBLIC CANNOT TELL US (so nobody spends slots on it)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Differences below 0.0007 between near-copies (every pool-of-pools, stacker, weight, aggregation or 4-root arm question).
- Anything about private beyond the hour/weekend reprojection: public has the private coverage regime already, and the extra
  1-2 days of staleness on private cannot be emulated by a public probe.
- Weekday-specific effects (private is 76% weekday): those are val's job (Tue/Thu) and the holdout's.
- Member-level selection: a per-member public readout costs 20+ slots for SE 0.0012 each (corr ~0.90).
