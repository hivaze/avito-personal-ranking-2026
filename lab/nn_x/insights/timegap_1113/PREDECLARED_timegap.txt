SOURCES (label-free, time-ordered): per item a 15-bit day mask over the train days (Sep 5-19 MSK) of (i) appearances as a

  A gap_last      D - last earlier day the item was a candidate (NaN if never)

  C gap_first     D - first earlier day the item was a candidate (NaN if never); report Spearman(gap_first, f4) on val
  D never_seen    1 if no earlier candidate appearance (= isnan(A))
  E n_days_active number of distinct earlier train days with a candidate appearance (0 if never)

ITEMS (one LIN each): A, B, C, D, E individually, and ALL4 = [A, B, C, E] as one block (4 x [z, pctrank, isnan]).
