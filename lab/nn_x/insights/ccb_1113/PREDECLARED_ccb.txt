B4 Residual model LIN (listwise softmax, multi-positive): s = beta*b + sum_j [ w_j1*zf(x_j) + w_j2*(pctrank_feed(x_j) - 0.5)
   + w_j3*isnan(x_j) ] (isnan term only when x_j has NaN), loss = -sum y*s + sum_feeds npos_f*log sum_feed exp(s),
   L-BFGS-B from beta = 0.94, others 0, maxiter 200 (s04 nll / fit_lin, unchanged). Control = the same fit on [b] alone.

   item). Per-feed monotone transforms of b (calibration, temperature, log p) are excluded from every construction: they are
   no-ops for per-feed NDCG. Feed-constant quantities (user aggregates) can enter only as interactions with candidate-level
   columns (C10); a feed-constant column alone is a no-op and is not tested.
B5 Nested val delta. Each fold's LIN and control are fitted on the other 4 folds and scored on the held-out fold; paired
   per-feed delta d_f = NDCG(LIN) - NDCG(control) over all 14,423 feeds; delta = mean d_f, SE = sd(d_f)/sqrt(14,423),
   z = delta/SE, one-sided p = 1 - Phi(z).

   EARLY holdout column, the s04 noise band (-0.00047..+0.00022) next to each delta, and 4 N(0,1) noise columns run once in

=====================================================================================================================
D. THE BAR (pre-declared; one read per item)
=====================================================================================================================
An item PASSES iff ALL of:
 (1) nested val delta (B5) >= +0.0005, AND
 (2) Holm-corrected one-sided p <= 0.02275 over the 16 items (step-down: sort the 16 p-values ascending; the k-th smallest
     is rejected iff p_(j) <= 0.02275/(16 - j + 1) for every j <= k). Equivalent z thresholds (k-th smallest p):
     k=1 2.984 | 2 2.964 | 3 2.943 | 4 2.920 | 5 2.895 | 6 2.868 | 7 2.837 | 8 2.803 | 9 2.765 | 10 2.721 | 11 2.670 |
     12 2.608 | 13 2.531 | 14 2.428 | 15 2.278 | 16 2.000. Items with no test (C15, any STOP) enter with p = 1.
 (3) holdout delta with the full-val weight (B6) >= 0 (point estimate), AND
 (4) LATE-holdout delta (Sep 17-19, 8,279 feeds) >= 0 (point estimate), AND
 (5) pmix (B7) >= -1 SE (veto only).
Everything else is CLOSED. EARLY column, slices and noise band are information.
A PASS means only "worth the item's next stage as written in checks.json (its own pre-declared GPU/NN rule)"; nothing is
pooled, fine-tuned, or submitted from this batch, and the frozen pick-1 rule is untouched.

E. KNOWN WEAKNESSES (DATA_PIPELINE_1113 §2, disclosed in advance)
- Positive controls of this residual lens recover only ~15% of a known input gap (§2.4): a CLOSED here closes the post-hoc
  residual form; it is weak evidence about training-time (NN-input) value. The synthesis must say so per item.
- Val-fitted residuals transfer negatively to the holdout (an uninformative tilt costs about -0.0002..-0.0004; 82/94 label-free
  constructions were negative there), so criterion (3) is conservative.
- Multiplicity: ~16 looks at nominal 2 SE would give ~0.3 false passes; Holm keeps the family-wise rate at 0.02275.
- The holdout is M >= 50 only and popularity-fresh; the late column is the closest holdout analogue of test timing.
