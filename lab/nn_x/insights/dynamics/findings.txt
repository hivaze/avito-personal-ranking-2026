5. THE RE-COOL VAL EXCESS: DECOMPOSITION AND MECHANISM (a2, a3, a7)
======================================================================================================================
What differs between a re-cool and the original cooldown (code-level, nn_x/r6/train.py): (i) only state_dict is loaded
-> AdamW moments and Muon momentum start at zero, warmup 0 (first AdamW steps are sign-like, size ~lr = 2e-3 per
coordinate on every AdamW parameter: embeddings incl. the domain token, LayerNorm, biases, the 1-output heads);
(ii) a fresh data permutation (seed 811/812/813) instead of epochs 8-9 of the run's own seed; (iii) 1180 fewer stable
steps (@77500 vs 78680); (iv) eval/selection grid 1000 vs 2500. The lr schedule is IDENTICAL (no lr jump).

Hypotheses:
  - lr jump: EXCLUDED (identical lr(k); the snapshot was taken at lr 2e-3).
  - data order: EXCLUDED as driver. Three seeds (811/812/813) and two snapshots (@70000, @77500) all show it; the shared

  - selection grid / luck: explains ~0.0005 of the +0.0013 measured vs the F7 originals, not the trajectory-level part.
  - snapshot state: @77500 is 0.0008 "val-low / hold-high" vs P (it is a late-epoch phase point, hold +0.00044); that
    biases AGAINST a val excess, so it is not the cause.

    the first eval (k=1000 change minus the original's 77.5k->80k change: gap -0.0005+-0.0006 / +0.0006+-0.0009),
    early-cooldown holdout lag of the re-cools (k 1.3-3.8k: hold mostly -0.0005..-0.0011 vs the original, val -0.0012..
    +0.0003; seed-811 arms ~0 on val, seed-813 arms -0.0007..-0.0012), the
    val-over-holdout offset is in place by k ~4-6k (early gap +0.0004..+0.0009 vs the originals' +0.00015) and then
    persists or grows. It travels with cold-candidate promotion (p5). A restart kick that un-learns in-period fine
    structure (which the in-period holdout uses and the post-period val does not) fits these shapes.
  Whether the excess transfers to test is NOT answerable from curves (the cold promotion does transfer as behaviour).

8. SCHEDULE IDEAS vs EVIDENCE (what the curves say; COVERAGE_MAP status)
======================================================================================================================
- m02 mix ramp (0 -> 4%) / front-loaded pool: COVERAGE_MAP has m02 (ADOPTED), m0 (CLOSED vs m02), mix .1 / .3, pp02
  (STOP), aug (STOP); no time-varying mix. Curves: m02 = m0 on val at every k (TL;DR 2), replay damage only after ~2
  passes at lr < ~6e-4. A ramp at equal dose (0.94 pass) moves the cold/warm balance only; expected val ~0, coldpos
  +-0.002. LOW priority (E6).
- Exploration share in the STABLE phase: tried in the old recipe as up-weighting exploration feeds in the main run
  (nn_report: -0.001). The stable phase is not where m02 acts. LOW, not proposed.
- Warmup length: NEVER varied (COVERAGE_MAP). First eval (2.5k) shows no lr-dependent damage (lr1 - lr2 val -0.00002).
  No signal; not proposed. (Warmup matters for RE-COOLS - see E2.)
- Cooldown lr floor (3%): NEVER. m02 re-cools still gain +0.0002-0.0003 val in the last 3.3k steps (lr < 2.5e-4), so
  raising the floor would cost; lowering it has <= +0.0001 room. Not proposed (except lr1-type families, E5 note).
- Muon momentum in the cooldown: NEVER. Late m02 jitter is already 0.0002; larger momentum = less gradient noise,
  and r7 found lower gradient noise harmful (batch x2 -0.0015). Not proposed.
- EMA/SWA of the cooldown tail: plateau averaging CLOSED (diversity). Tail bound <= +0.0001 per member (jitter 0.0002-
  0.0004 at lr < 2e-4; scaling the plateau avg3 result +0.0007 at jitter 0.0008 by variance), and it would push member
  corr further up. Not proposed.
- Checkpoint selection by a val-like criterion: under m02 the selection position is ~the end anyway; what matters is
  using the end (no selection) and keeping the holdout clean (E3). A cold-aware holdout criterion would need per-eval
  holdout predictions (not logged) - not worth it now.
- Longer/shorter cooldown: CLOSED (wave 3). Earlier cooldown as a COST lever (COVERAGE_MAP P1, OPEN): late plateau adds
  only +0.0006-0.0010 at P over 3.8 epochs; F7x70 (@70000) vs F7x: -0.0004 val / 0 hold (partly the bad snapshot phase).
  Compute is not the binding constraint for the next 4 weeks, so not proposed.
