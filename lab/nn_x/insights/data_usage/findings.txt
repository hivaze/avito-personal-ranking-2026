TRAINING-DATA USAGE AND POSITION BIAS - findings (data_usage analyst, 2026-11-12, CPU only)
=======================================================================================
Dir: <repo>/nn_x/insights/data_usage (scripts 01-15, out/*.csv|parquet|log, out/gb/ = GBDT proxy cache,
results and the Optuna study). No GPU used, no file outside this directory written. Numbers are reproducible from the
scripts. "L" = per-feed listwise CE / log M (the RHO scoring quantity; 1.0 = uniform scores). "snap" = F7 @77500
snapshots r101-r104 (the CD --init, scored by rho_score.py with the TRAINING domain token); "ref" = the pool-only
reference r6_rhoref_pool_s1 (134K random-like M>=50 feeds, 4 epochs; val 0.38199). Label-defined slices (cold/warm
positive, rank of the positive) are descriptive, not causal.

TL;DR
1. Signal mix. 95.7% of the NN's gradient units are production feeds (position-biased labels); random-like feeds are
   4.3% in the main phase and 5.65% in the m02 cooldown (+0.65 replay passes over 134K feeds). K9 (production-order
   aux) acts on 95% of units. ~214K exploration feeds hidden among the 1.6M short (M<50) feeds are trained as
   production (production domain token, K9 on a random order).
2. Position bias is about HALF examination, half production relevance. In random-like train feeds the click lift at
   rank 0 is 2.5-2.8x (examination); in production 4.5-6.8x; the examination share of the log-lift is 0.5-0.6 at every
   rank and M bin. Calibrated propensities are mild (1/theta 0.33-1.5, ESS 87%); the XGBoost propensities that
   "failed" earlier were ~2.5x too steep in log (confounded with the ranker's relevance).
3. "Imitation". About half (0.51) of the trained snapshot's production-train advantage over a production-blind
   (pool-only) reference does not exist on val (L_ref-L_snap 0.0265 vs 0.0131, M-matched; upper bound). The snapshot
   agrees with production order 1.2-1.9x more steeply than the reference. The category pattern of the advantage
   transfers to val (corr 0.86); the cold-positive part does not (production shows 12% cold candidates, ranker-
   selected; exploration 21%; val 38%). The main phase buys warm positives (ref->snap +0.0137 warm, +0.0023 all-cold);
   the cooldown + replay buys cold positives (snap->F7x +0.0148 all-cold, +0.0007 warm).
4. Production agreement is VALUE, not bias. Every lever that removes or down-weights production-agreeing feeds hurts,
   NN and proxy alike: NN rho70 cooldown -0.0035 val (z -5.7) / -0.0020 holdout per member (official readout, 4 roots;
   damage already at step 1000, on warm positives and short feeds); GBDT proxy rho70 -0.0041 / -0.0037 (calibrates the proxy), calibrated IPS -0.0058 / -0.0048, drop
   top-slot-positive feeds -0.0082 / -0.0087, inverse selection (drop hardest) -0.006. Learnability curricula, IPS and
   any "de-bias by selection" are closed. Recency oversampling (rec33) loses -0.0021 val, all of it on cold positives
   (-0.0084), via late-cooldown memorisation (same signature as mix 0.1): keep any cooldown subset at <= ~+1 extra pass.
5. The ONE position correction that helps is end-of-feed (truncation) handling: a fixed training-only offset on the
   true rank-from-end 0-2 gives +0.0022..+0.0024 val (z 3.4-3.8) / +0.0020..+0.0022 holdout in the calibrated proxy
   (3 seeds per arm), all from production feeds (a production feed's last item is a production-deep item clicked at 23.5%: an anti-production
   confound). It was NEVER tested in the NN: every NN/MLP position tower indexed rank-from-end as M-1-raw pos, which
   with the gaps (85.5% of feeds) puts 13.5 candidates/feed into "last" with a 2.6% click rate instead of 23.5%.
   This is the top proposal (E1); NN-ready rkend arrays and lift table are in out/nn_artifacts/.
6. Optuna (36 TPE trials over exam offset, trunc offset, IPS clip, pool weight, learnability drop; pre-declared
   TUNE/CONFIRM split of val) picks trunc 1.2 + exam 0.4 + pool weight 0.56 and never IPS or dropping. The best
   trial does NOT confirm (CONFIRM +0.0019 +- 0.0012, z 1.6; TUNE +0.0039): it is no better than the plain truncation
   offset. Optuna's robust message is the negative one (IPS / any learnability drop = worst region).
7. Proposed next (section 7): E1 truncation offset in the NN (2-epoch screen -> CD arm), E2 a 10-GPU-min domain-token
   diagnostic, E4 front-loaded replay at equal dose; E3/E5 conditional / low.

  (full table in out/03_randtopn.csv; the examination share is 0.5-0.6 at every rank above ~30 and every M bin)

- Pooled examination curve theta_rand (relative to the feed base, M>=50 random-like): rank 0 3.08, 1 2.51, 2 1.93,
  5 1.62, 10 1.53, 20 1.34, 50 1.09, 100 0.77, 200 0.66 (out/theta_rand_rank.npy). The production CTR curve is 1.6-2x
  as steep in log: 6.13, 3.86, 2.83, 2.13, 1.76, 1.35, 0.96, 0.68, 0.52 (out/ctr_prod_rank.npy).
- Consequences:
  (a) An examination-only correction moves 15.1% of the production positives' slot mass (total-variation distance) and
      halves the top-3 share (15.0% -> 7.3%).
  (b) Proper inverse-propensity weights are mild: 1/theta = 0.33 (rank 0) ... 0.66 (10) ... 1.29 (100) ... 1.51 (200);
      ESS 87% of the production positives. The XGBoost "unbiased LambdaMART" propensities that failed earlier
      (deep_gbdt_ranking.md 2.3: 0.47 at pos 1, 0.15 at pos 10, 0.028 at pos 100 => weights 7-50x, IPW -0.0022) were
      ~2.5x too steep in log (theta_rand gives 0.50 at rank 10 and 0.25 at rank 100 relative to rank 0): they absorbed the production ranker's relevance (the confounding of Google KDD'23), so that
      failure says nothing about a correctly calibrated correction.
  (c) The old LightGBM offset (src/train_lgb.py --pos_mode offset, alpha 1 x log production CTR(pos); tr300k_offset
      0.3670 vs tr300k_none 0.3679) used the confounded curve, i.e. it removed the relevance half too.
- Holdout control (08_holdout_position.py; 30K random-like feeds, single positive): a position-blind model finds
  top-slot positives HARDER than deep ones in random order (M 50-100: L 0.927 at ranks 1-2, 0.932 at 3-9, 0.918 at
  10-49, 0.900 at >=50; same pattern in every M bin). That is the position-based-model signature: a click at a
  high-attention slot needs less relevance. Last-item positives are EASIER (L 0.894 vs 0.920, M 50-100; NDCG 0.359 vs
  0.328): the truncation spike marks "found it and left" clicks, not noise. Model rank vs shown rank in holdout feeds:
  Spearman +0.005 +- 0.001 for posterior <= 0.95 vs -0.018 for > 0.995 (residual production contamination of the
  least-certain holdout feeds; small).

  val-weighted 0.0265                  0.0131              0.506
  => about half of what the production-trained trunk "knows" on production feeds does not exist under random order.
  This is an upper bound on imitation: it also contains in-sample fit (the snapshot trained on these feeds, the
  reference did not), time shift, and whatever the TRAINING domain token carries (L_snap was scored with it; E2
  measures that part). In NDCG terms the transferable half is worth ref -> snap +0.0101 +- 0.0011 val.
3.2 Imitation slope (single-positive production feeds, last-item excluded): dL / d[log(1+rank of positive)/log M]
  snap / ref = 1.21 (M<20), 1.49 (20-50), 1.74 (50-100), 1.89 (100-200), 1.93 (200-300). The production-trained model
  agrees with the production order beyond what a production-blind relevance model does, increasingly with M.
  Memorisation would flatten this slope (it lowers the loss most on hard, deep-positive feeds), so the excess slope is a
  lower bound on order imitation.
3.3 What transfers (val, random order) and what does not:
  - By category of the positive the advantage TRANSFERS: corr(adv_val, adv_prod) = 0.86 over the 15 largest
    categories (val ~ half of prod in level; out/10_val_adv_pcat.csv). Production data teaches real category-level
    relevance.
  - By coldness it does NOT: on production feeds the snapshot's advantage is largest for all-cold-positive feeds
    (0.051 vs 0.027 warm), on val it is smallest (all-cold 0.0068 +- 0.0018 vs warm 0.0169 +- 0.0010; NDCG ref->snap
    all-cold +0.0023 +- 0.0022 n.s., warm +0.0137 +- 0.0015).
  - Why (11_cold_exposure.py): production feeds show few, ranker-selected cold items: cold (it_hist_all_luo == 0)
    candidate share 12.3% production M>=50 vs 20.7% random-like train feeds vs 37.9% val; shown cold items in
    production are better than average (cold/warm click ratio 0.67 production vs 0.59 random-like; val 0.71 with its
    own cold definition). Production does not push cold items to the top (cold share flat ~11% over ranks).

5.2 Reading.
  (a) Every lever that re-weights or selects production feeds AGAINST production agreement hurts, in proportion to how
      hard it pushes: droptop -0.008 > ips3 -0.006 > rho70 -0.004 (NN official -0.0035) > rnd70 (pure size) -0.0015. Even
      correctly calibrated, mild IPS (weights 0.33-1.5, ESS 87%) costs -0.006. Top-slot production positives are the
      single most valuable training feeds for val. The production ranker's ordering is mostly signal for us: keep using
      it as a teacher (K9) and do not de-bias it away. Selection in the OTHER direction (rhoinv: drop the hardest
      feeds) also hurts, mostly on short feeds: removing feeds by learnability in either direction loses information.
  (b) The one position correction that helps is END-OF-FEED (truncation) handling: a fixed offset for rank-from-end
      0-2, +0.0022..+0.0024 val (z 3.4-3.8) and +0.0020..+0.0022 holdout, robust to alpha 0.5-1.2 (Optuna), and it stops the
      base model's decay with more rounds (trunc1 0.3735 at 500 rounds vs base 0.3695). The gain sits in PRODUCTION
      feeds (tprod1 +0.0022 / +0.0021 holdout vs trand1 +0.0006 / -0.0006): in a production feed the last shown item is, by construction, a
      production-DEEP item clicked at a 23.5% rate (truncation), so without the offset the model learns "features of
      items the production ranker puts last are good" - an anti-production confound that is pure noise on val.
      Dropping those feeds instead (droplast) is neutral: the feeds carry useful information elsewhere; only the
      position-explained part of their label must be explained away.
  (c) The examination (RandTopN) offset is weakly positive (+0.0006..+0.0008 val, +0.0012..+0.0015 holdout, n.s. on
      val) and adds nothing on top of the truncation offset (exam1+trunc1 +0.0018 vs trunc1 +0.0023). The confounded
      production-CTR offset (the old tr300k_offset design) is negative (-0.0006, -0.0024 on M>=50): removing the
      relevance half of the slope hurts.
  (d) Exploration data: more distinct random-like feeds help about as much as any extra data (addX +0.0011 for +20%
      feeds; rnd70 size effect -0.0015 for -21%); up-weighting them hurts (pool3 -0.0005; Optuna prefers w_pool ~0.56).
      The NN-style domain input helps the train-period random-like holdout (+0.0018) but not val (+0.0005);
      with the truncation offset the holdout gain adds up (dom+trunc1 holdout +0.0039) while val stays at +0.0021.
  (e) Cold-rich (label-free) up-weighting: neutral on val (-0.0001), +0.0009 holdout. Not a lever.

5.3 Why the NN never saw the truncation gain (14_rev_index_check.py). Every NN / MLP position tower in this project
  indexed rank-from-end as rev = clamp(M-1-pos, 0) with the RAW shown position (nn/model.py forward; solvers
  loss_zoo.py:202). 85.5% of train feeds have gaps (removed already-viewed items, mean 7.9 per feed), so the index-0
  bucket holds 13.5 candidates per production M>=50 feed with a 2.6% click rate instead of the true last item's 23.5%
  (prod M<50: 3.4 candidates, 9.1% vs 12.1%; random-like: 4.7 candidates, 6.8% vs 28.4%). The end-of-feed component
  of the tower (rev_aux -0.0010, nn_report round 1; base vs nopos -0.0002; MLP 'pos' -0.0006) was therefore never a
  test of truncation handling. The GBDT results that use the TRUE rank-from-end (EDA b4: rkend 57% of gain, +0.0042;
  LightGBM position GAM +0.0022 / +0.0024 here; fixed offset +0.0023) all agree. Ready inputs for an NN arm:
  out/nn_artifacts/train_rkend_true.npy, train_rank_true.npy (int16, flat train order) and trunc_lift.json
  (log lifts: prod M<50 rkend0 1.00 / rkend1 0.54 / rkend2 2.01; prod M>=50 2.81 / -0.20 / -0.22; random-like
  3.31 / 0.04 / 0.03).

5.4 Optuna (09_optuna_usage.py, user's request "try things like optuna if needed"). Pre-declared: TPE over alpha_exam
  [0,1.5], alpha_trunc [0,1.5], ips_clip {off,1.5,2,3}, w_pool [0.5,4] (log), drop_hi [0,0.3]; 36 trials (3 workers x 12,
  seed 0, 400 rounds), objective = TUNE half of val (user hash); CONFIRM half untouched; best trial re-fit with seeds 1-2

E1  TRUNC-NN: true rank-from-end (truncation) offset in the NN training loss            [priority 1]
    Mechanism: production feeds end right after the last click, so the last shown item - a production-DEEP item - is
      positive 23.5% of the time (M>=50; 12-33% at rkend 0/2 in short feeds). A position-blind model learns
      "features of items the ranker puts last are good" (anti-production noise). A fixed training-only logit offset
      a*L[group, rkend] for rkend 0-2 (group = prod M<50 / prod M>=50 / random-like; L from
      out/nn_artifacts/trunc_lift.json) explains those clicks away; inference unchanged. Variant: 12 learned scalars
      (group x rkend 0/1/2/>=3), i.e. the old rev tower correctly indexed and coarse.
    Protocol: guarded flag in a NEW cd_arms5.py (flag-off bit-identity as for wave 4) that loads
      out/nn_artifacts/train_rkend_true.npy into the batch (b["rkend"]); listwise logits + offset on training batches
      only (not the K9 aux, not eval/predict). Stage A: 2-epoch from-scratch paired screen (r7 harness, seeds 23/24), fixed
      a=1 vs base; read val, holdout, M<50/M>=50. Stage B (A >= 0 on val and holdout): CD re-cool from @77500 with m02 +
      offset in the cooldown, roots 101-104 vs m02 (wave-4 rules). Stage C (B passes): from-scratch 10-epoch members.
    Expected: +0.0005..+0.0015 per member (proxy +0.0024 val / +0.0020 holdout, stable over alpha 0.5-1.2); P ~0.35.
    Cost: A ~1.5 GPU-h, B ~2 GPU-h, C ~4.5 GPU-h per member. Kill: A pooled d_val < 0 or d_hold < 0; B stage-1 futility.
    Already tried: only MIS-INDEXED towers: g(pos)+g'(M-1-pos) in nn/model.py (round 1: base 0.37361 vs nopos 0.37385;
      rev_aux -0.0010, nn_report.md), MLP proxy 'pos' -0.0006 (sol_solvers 2.1, loss_zoo.py:202); COVERAGE_MAP lists
      "position tower CLOSED (old recipe)". Correct rank-from-end exists only in GBDT: EDA b4 +0.0042 (rkend 57% of gain),
      LightGBM position GAM +0.0022 (deep_gbdt_ranking 2.3), this proxy +0.0023. Never in the NN.
