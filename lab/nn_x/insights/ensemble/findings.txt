0. TL;DR
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. The premise needs a correction: the FT half did not stop adding because of m02. At pool level it is ~0 for EVERY
   pre2s-era family, including the c0-cooled F7 (-0.00019 +- 0.00039) and F7b (+0.00019), and for the m02 re-cools
   (F7x +0.00036, F7x70 +0.00002) and NEW (V27g -0.00021, V28l -0.00005, V29d -0.00010). It still adds for the cvnd-era
   families (F5cd +0.00096, F6 +0.00151) and for un-cooled pre2s plateau trunks (F7p +0.00094). Two things killed it:
   pre2s in the trunk and the cooldown. m02 only shrank the member-level number further.
2. Why the member-level FT gain does not reach the pool. Infinite-member limits (a4_ainf.csv): the FT branch's a_inf
   is 0.0003-0.0006 BELOW the TO branch's for every pre2s-era set (G3 -0.00042, F7x -0.00049, F7b -0.00057, F7 -0.00032,
   V28l -0.00050). The final 0.5/0.5 limit equals the TO limit (+0.00005, +0.00005, -0.00003, -0.00007, -0.00009). For
   the cvnd families FT's limit is above TO's (F5cd +0.00098, F6 +0.00019; final +0.00086 / +0.00102).
   So on modern trunks the single-member FT gain (+0.0005-0.0010) is variance reduction, which the 20-member pool
   already provides. FT also homogenises the members: mean pairwise member corr goes 0.945 -> 0.957 (G3, val and test
   alike), and the pool-over-mean-member gain drops from +0.00440 (TO) to +0.00334 (FT).
3. The pool-level FT effect is strongly M-dependent and replicates. For G3 it is M<20 -0.0024 +- 0.0011, M20-49 -0.0005,
   M50-99 +0.0005, M100-199 +0.0007, M200+ +0.0014. Using TO only for M<50 feeds (FT half kept for M>=50) gives:
   - G3 +0.00054 +- 0.00028, PF7x +0.00063 +- 0.00029, M2 +0.00043, G3+NEW +0.00044;
   - 11 of 11 pre2s/m02 families >= 0 (mean +0.00027). The single-member V30f datapoints are noise-level: s901
     +0.00036; s902 -0.00009, fresh, checked after the declaration;
   - the two cvnd families negative (F5cd -0.00042, F6 -0.00068), which explains why the 11-11 M-split test
     (sol_ensemble §2.2 S1) did not replicate.
   A concrete mechanism: n2cpv3 trains with loss_norm 'batch' (cand_budget 8192, max_feeds 128). An M~300 feed then
   gets ~4.9x the gradient weight of an M<=64 feed, whereas the trunk uses equal per-feed weight. nn_report's K1 note
   ("neutral in FT because val batches are small and M varies less") is wrong for these settings: the skew is the same
   4.9x as in the train stage.
4. The m02 hypothesis is rejected at the vector level, with one partial exception (shift vectors, a3 (3)):
   - The FT shift s_FT = z(FT pool) - z(TO pool) is a common correction: across families it correlates 0.75-0.94
     (val and test).
   - It is ORTHOGONAL to the m02 shift z(F7x TO) - z(F7b TO): r between -0.07 and +0.15.
   - The one shared component is the cold-item push. FT's mean shift on cold candidates shrinks with cooling and then
     with m02: F7p +0.063 -> F7 +0.029 -> F7b +0.022 -> F7x +0.013 (warm: -0.025 / -0.012 / -0.009 / -0.005).
   - At pool level FT still trades warm for cold: G3 coldpos +0.0051, warmpos -0.0017 (label-defined, descriptive).
5. What FT still uniquely does is small and now mostly history-similarity recalibration:
   - The FT shift is only 13-22% explained (R2) by the 42 extras.
   - For the cvnd families its top correlates are the pre_* encoder scores (pre_* group R2 0.11-0.12). For pre2s
     trunks this falls to 0.03-0.06, because pre2s absorbed it. Within F5/F5cd, the prew-in-trunk members have a
     smaller member FT gain (+0.0011 / +0.0013 vs +0.0018 / +0.0018).
   - The FT shift now loads on seq_max_all / seq_top3 / ctx_nd_max (r +0.21-0.26). The production cooldown moves the
     opposite way (cool(F7-F7p) r -0.17 with the same features).
   - The transductive channel (items clicked by other users in other val folds) is negligible: 0.4% of val candidates,
     0.3% of test. Removing the FT shift on them changes the pool by -0.00002.
6. Blend-level fixes other than the M-split are closed. All evaluated nested over the FT user folds:
   - global w_ft: G3 +0.00018 +- 0.00022, PF7x +0.00005, M2 -0.00014;
   - Optuna TPE per-family (FT, TO) weights on G3+NEW: +0.00008 +- 0.00034 (in-fold optimism +0.0004 to +0.0011);
   - "FT on top of the pool" as a linear listwise residual on the 42 extras over G3: all 8 variants -0.0001 to -0.0005;
   - transplanting other families' FT shifts: inconsistent signs.
7. Ensemble behaviour:
   - Per-feed disagreement is seed noise, not structure. The per-feed oracle over different families equals the
     oracle over same-family seeds (+0.042 vs +0.045 at k=3, +0.072 vs +0.071 at k=6).
   - No family wins high-disagreement feeds beyond its average. Per-segment family selection, nested, is <= +0.0009
     (best H, z 1.96, 1 of 7 variables).
   - Test disagreement equals val (members 0.0548 vs 0.0553) and public equals private (0.0548 / 0.0548).
   - Adding any existing family, NEW included, to G3 is <= 0 on val and on holdout (G3+NEW: val -0.00013, holdout
     -0.00029).
   - Infinite-member headroom of the current pools is <= +0.0003 (G3 val +0.00013 / holdout -0.00017; M2 +0.00024 /
     +0.00001; all 55 modern members kn 0.40019 / 0.32344, headroom <= +0.0001). More members of existing recipes
     cannot move the pool.
8. FT recipe facts that matter for the ftopt study:
   - OOF peaks at epoch 1 in every family. The saved EMA-final is below epoch 1 by F5cd 0.00015, F7 0.00021, F7x
     0.00044, F7b 0.00060, V29d 0.00072, V28l 0.00075. The better-cooled trunks overfit val faster.
   - Where FT and TO disagree most (top decile), TO is right more often (TO>FT 0.39-0.40 vs FT>TO 0.35-0.36) and the
     final loses 0.0015-0.0030.
   - Fine-tuning 3 members of G3 reproduces fine-tuning all 20 (+0.00001 +- 0.00022). k=1: -0.00058; k=2: -0.00027.

5. CAVEATS
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- All FT numbers are val OOF. On test the FT half is a 5-fold mean and moves test less than val. There is no holdout FT,
  so FT conclusions rest on val alone (paired SE ~0.0003-0.0004 at pool level).
- The cross-family replications (M-split 11/11, a_inf 5/5) are robustness across trunks on the SAME val feeds, not
  independent samples. The pool-level SE (~0.00028) is the honest uncertainty. E1 therefore needs the fresh-member
  confirmation.
- The a_inf extrapolation assumes the 1/k law. Mixed pools (G3, ALL55) fit it less well, so their headroom figures are
  approximate (+-0.0002).
- Re-cooled members carry a procedural val-over-holdout excess (~+0.001, CDSEED-813). Val levels of F7x / F7x70 / G3
  are flattered; this does not affect the FT-vs-TO comparisons, which are within-member.
- V30f had 1 member at the a6 run (2 at a8). Proper V30 numbers need s903-906.
