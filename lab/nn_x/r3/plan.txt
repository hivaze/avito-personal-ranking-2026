Reading: the encoder's item-identity memory, trained on next-feed-open, is popularity-aligned: in exactly the cell where an
identity signal must help it prefers the same popular same-category beaters that G3 prefers, and the ranker (which already takes
the pre2s scalars as inputs) leaves no residual. Initialising the history encoder from it would transfer that bias, not new
information; the critique's own kill rule ("zero-shot gain < +0.005 ... stop before plumbing") applies.
