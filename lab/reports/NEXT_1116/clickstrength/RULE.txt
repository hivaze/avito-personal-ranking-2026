3. THE STATISTIC AND THE TWO FORMS (sign on record: +)

     position weight w_r(pos) = (clicks_r[pos] + 20 x rate_r) / (rows_r[pos] + 20) over the non-holdout train feeds of regime r on slots 0-4 (before every row with a view).
     for a row j of source feed f:  q_j = npos_f x w_r(pos_j) / sum over the rows k of f of w_r(pos_k)      (expected clicks given the feed's click count; BT score at theta = 0)
     per item i:  c_i = clicks, e_i = sum of q, over S.
     shrinkage a = clip(1 / tau2, 0.5, 50), tau2 = sum_i[(c_i - e_i)^2 - e_i] / sum_i e_i^2 over the items of window {0, 1, 2} (a = 50 if tau2 <= 0); ONE constant per form.
