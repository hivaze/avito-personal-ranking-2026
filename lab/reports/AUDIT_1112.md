What we are missing (ranked by the size of the evidence):
1. **TRUNC-PROD is the only lever with member-level evidence ≥ +0.002** (GBDT proxy +0.0022 ± 0.0007 val / +0.0021 holdout, replicated +0.0031 ± 0.0006 / +0.0020 ± 0.0005 on fresh seeds, growing with training). It has two never-run siblings with the same mechanism: a training-only **listing-age offset on production rows** (avito: production over-exposes fresh listings by +0.89 nats at < 3 h; the final pool still over-ranks them, act/exp 0.86) and a correctly indexed **pos + rkend tower** (eda_labels_position b4 +0.0042 vs rkend-only).
2. **A never-measured mis-specification: 51 % of train feeds (M < 50) are hard-labelled PRODUCTION** (nn/train.py:160) for the domain token and the full-weight K9 aux, and are excluded from the cooldown pool and the holdout. 409 K of them (25.5 % of M < 50, 13 % of all train feeds) have zero pos-gaps and a random-like position curve (rank-0 lift 1.00 at M < 20 vs 4.74 for production M ≥ 50), so the K9 aux distils a random order into ~13 % of the stream, in the M < 50 regime that is 39 % of test feeds and 34 % of G3's loss and that no holdout sees (weakspots t10; INSIGHTS L6 had it as "idle lane only").
3. **The popularity family is the only input family that is both relied on and shifted toward private**, and its private-like cost is localised: the 4-member F7x TO pool loses −0.00175 ± 0.00053 val (−0.00227 ± 0.00081 pmix) under private-like coverage, concentrated in feeds whose positives are 7-24 days old (−0.0092 ± 0.0014); the CPU replay by family shows the loss is entirely st_pop (−0.00124 / −0.00145) and pre2 contributes ± 0.0001. The joint age × coverage shift (LUO zero rate of 3-7-day listings: train 0.12-0.23, val 0.62, public 0.83, private 0.86) is invisible to marginal adversarial validation (features s02c, s06). This upgrades the hzcd parity lever to private-specific and re-targets it at 7-24-day listings.
4. **The FT branch adds nothing at pool level on val** (blend − TO-only −0.00003 ± 0.00039; M < 50 −0.0013, M ≥ 50 +0.0009) and has never been isolated on the LB (all 10 entries are 0.5/0.5 blends). One TO-only submission is a cheap, decisive forward-transfer test (weakspots t7/t4).
5. **Measurement limits.** The holdout ranks families (rho 0.93 over 170 members) but not seeds/checkpoints (within-family rho(holdout, val) +0.33; rho(holdout, val M < 50) +0.20; val M < 50 vs M ≥ 50 agree at +0.26); seed-level val differences (sd 0.0005-0.0012) are mostly noise; nothing but the over-used val measures M < 50. Three +2 SE nested combiner effects (M-split, S2, raw pooling) failed fresh-member replication within 24 h; the registry's CLOSED means "not large at screen power (~+0.0008 detectable)", not null (weakspots t2/t5b; COVERAGE_MAP).
6. **Operational single point of failure.** No git, no backup of preds/ (21 GB, the only copy of every member's FT predictions; FT fold models are never saved), every pick/decision script lives only in the /tmp scratchpad; rules 8.6.3 require a report + materials check for the top 10; the number of selectable final solutions is not stated in the rules (weakspots t6).
7. **The one never-tried structural item** (altapproach R3): the pretrained sequence encoder (8 M dense, 2 epochs) reaches the ranker only through 9 scalar features (+0.0009); its weights were never used to initialise the ranker's history encoder, and the pretraining was never scaled. Its CPU gate on the scalar version failed today (cell AUC 0.477 / 0.470 vs 0.53), so P ~0.1; it is the only candidate for a "#1 has a better model" world.

## 4. Avito nuances that matter

Source: `nn_x/audit/avito/findings.txt` (web: habr 1090584 / 1086210 / 1004694 / 974682 / 1069352, kod.ru 2026; competition page; data checks a01-a08).

1. **Nothing public explains #1.** The competition page states only "val/test = feeds whose candidates were uniformly shuffled before display" and "target = ≥ 1 event of the target set, equal weights"; no public repo above 0.352; Telegram / ODS discussion not indexed; DF25 Avito-track talks login-walled. The only stated test-time channel is the one already known.
2. **Avito's published architecture is what the data shows, and is already exploited.** Item vector = attribute + text two-tower (category, location, price, seller, delivery flag, title / description / infomodel tokens; NO images); user vector = online per-request snapshot over listing actions + search queries; dot product is the top SHAP feature in production; f4 = exact listing age; s1 = recs-feed surface; the target is "an interaction started from this feed" (83 % type-1/source-1). Avito's "exploration" means mixing attention items, never shuffling; hides are the only negative feedback and are not in our events (every event type's same-category lift is 1.17-1.38; type 10 is a post-view engagement on the same item, 95.5 % co-occurring with a type-1 view).
3. **Listing age is the one platform nuance with a measurable residual** (the AGE-PROD mechanism, plan item 7): on shuffled traffic positive-rate lift by age is 0.29× (< 1 h), 0.37× (1-3 h), 0.51×, 0.60×, 0.73× (12-24 h), 0.89× (1-2 d), ~1.06-1.13× (> 1 week); production feeds show the opposite for fresh listings (lift 1.38 at pos 0-4; fresh listings reach the feed in ≤ 25 min). The final G3 pool: act/exp 0.875 / 0.859 / 0.979 / 1.040 / 0.996 / 1.076 / 1.097 / 1.031 / 1.068 / ~1.0 by age bin. A cross-fitted post-hoc 8-bin offset is worth only +0.00029 ± 0.00032 (M ≥ 50 +0.00049 ± 0.00036): the miscalibration sits on 2-5 % of rows. Public and private age shares differ by < 0.6 pp per bin (private has 1.4 pp more < 3 h listings).
4. **The candidate SET is blender-shaped even though the ORDER is shuffled.** No category quota (M ≥ 100: max per-category count median 59, q99 199, no mass points), but the candidate category mix tracks the history mix (within-feed corr 0.75 val / 0.76 test / 0.80 random-like train / 0.78 production; 86 % of candidates in a history category). Category share lift 0.29× (< 2 %) → 1.48× (> 70 %); nested over G3 +0.00015 ± 0.00025 (already used via feed_cat_share).
5. **Page boundary.** Val/test M histograms spike at M = 30/31 (1.6× / 1.9× neighbours), absent in train (1.1×); production M = 31 feeds end at max pos 30 (a 31-slot first page) and show the end-of-feed click spike over the last 4-5 slots (7-12 % vs 2.5 %). On val, G3 on M 29-31 feeds (0.443 ± 0.010) is monotone between M 26-28 (0.476) and 32-35 (0.436): nothing exploitable; it is the same truncation effect TRUNC-PROD handles on the training side.
6. **event_source is the UI surface of type-1/4/7 actions**: type-1 views 64.5 % s1 (recs feed), 23.7 % s2 (search; 0.25 categories/event vs 0.16; contact follow-up 8.8 % vs 5.1 %), 4.3 % s0. A category seen ONLY via search views is not a feed-click signal (lift 0.91 vs 1.13 feed-only, 1.19 both, 0.52 neither), but source-specific affinities carry nothing over G3 (c1_s1_20 +0.00018 ± 0.00015, c1_s2_20 −0.00016 ± 0.00011, cos to the search centroid −0.00036 ± 0.00018, hurts): the history encoder already has type + source tokens per event.
7. **Seasonality is composition.** Val positive rate by hour bucket 1.55 / 1.62 / 1.66 / 1.47 % (night / morning / day / evening); G3 NDCG 0.394 / 0.406 / 0.409 / 0.385 tracks mean M (101 / 93 / 92 / 105). Public is 100 % weekend and 56 % before noon; private 24 % weekend and 37 % evening. Category × hour / weekend priors: −0.00003 ± 0.00009 (val), −0.00001 ± 0.00006 (holdout, 32 % weekend feeds).
8. **Re-posting leaves no trace.** Age-days 26..34 per mille 6.3 6.2 6.2 5.8 4.4 4.5 4.4 4.3: no mass point at the 30-day re-post limit; re-posts get new item_ids and are only linkable via near-duplicate vectors (already captured by maxsim features; near-duplicate oracle +0.016 unchanged).
9. **Harness caveat for every residual test:** a LightGBM stacker with G3 as an input loses 0.0136 ± 0.0010 to raw G3; boosting FROM G3 as init_score reproduces it within −0.0003 ± 0.0003 but adding any feature set, even 12 the NN already uses, gives −0.0050 ± 0.0008 (tree residuals overfit 11.5 K-feed folds). Only nested additive tests and paired set-vs-set differences on the same residual harness are readable at ± 0.0003 (`nn_x/audit/avito/common_av.py`).
